Configuring your AI follow-up sequences

Set response times, write message templates, tune tone settings, and map each lead source to the right sequence — so the AI always says the right thing at the right time.

1. Setting response time

Business hours mode: Enable this to pause after-hours responses if you serve a market where late-night texts feel inappropriate. Leads will be queued and sent the next morning at your specified start time (e.g., 8:00 AM local time).

2. Customizing message templates

Templates are the foundation of your follow-up sequences. Each template uses dynamic variables (wrapped in {{curly braces}}) pulled from your CRM fields.

Example: Initial outreach SMS template

# Message 1 — Sent immediately on new lead Hey {{first_name}}, this is {{agent_name}} from {{business_name}}. I saw you were interested in {{service_type}} — did you have a quick minute to chat today or tomorrow? 📅

Example: 24-hour follow-up if no response

# Message 2 — Sent if no reply after 24 hours Hi {{first_name}}, just wanted to follow up! I know things get busy. Happy to answer any questions about {{service_type}} whenever works for you. What's your availability look like? 🗓️

Example: 72-hour re-engagement

# Message 3 — Sent 72 hours after message 2 if still no reply {{first_name}}, last reach out — I don't want to bother you, but I do have a slot open {{day_of_week}}. If now's not the right time, no worries at all. Just let me know! 🙌

Available variables: {{first_name}}, {{last_name}}, {{business_name}}, {{agent_name}}, {{service_type}}, {{lead_source}}, {{city}}, {{day_of_week}}, {{calendar_link}}, {{phone}}. Custom variables can be added by your implementation team.

3. Tone settings

Tone controls how the AI writes dynamic, unscripted responses when a lead replies. Choose from presets or define a custom tone profile for your brand.

Friendly & Casual

Uses contractions, occasional emojis, short sentences. Best for B2C: fitness, home services, insurance, real estate.

Professional & Direct

Formal language, no emojis, structured. Best for B2B: SaaS, consulting, finance, legal services.

Warm & Empathetic

Acknowledges feelings, slower pacing, more questions. Best for health, wellness, and high-ticket services.

Urgent & Action-Focused

Strong CTAs, scarcity language, direct asks. Best for short sales cycles and promotional campaigns.

Custom tone: Paste 5–10 examples of your own messages and our team will build a custom tone profile that makes the AI sound exactly like you. Share these during your strategy call or email them to ansh@nova-ops.space with subject line "Tone Examples."

4. Lead source mapping

Different lead sources need different follow-up approaches. A Facebook ad lead who clicked on "Get a free quote" needs a different message than a Google organic lead who found your website.

Referral Warm intro, mentions referrer's name 15–30 minutes

Common mistakes to avoid

⚠️

Sending too many messages too fast

Back-to-back messages within minutes of each other trigger spam filters and feel aggressive. Space messages at least 2–4 hours apart after the initial contact. For day 3+, space 24–48 hours minimum.

⚠️

Generic templates with no personalization

Messages that don't use the lead's first name or reference what they inquired about get 60% lower response rates. Always include at minimum {{first_name}} and {{service_type}}.

⚠️

Not mapping lead sources to separate sequences

A single universal sequence applied to all lead sources performs significantly worse than source-specific sequences. Take the time to map at least your top 3 lead sources to unique flows.

⚠️

Stopping too early

Most leads need 5–8 touches before they respond. Many businesses give up after 2–3 messages. A 30-day nurture sequence dramatically increases total conversions from cold leads.

⚠️

No clear call to action

Every message should have exactly one CTA — either "book a call," "reply to this message," or "click this link." Messages with multiple or no CTAs confuse leads and reduce reply rates.
