The Challenges

Why roofing companies leave money on the table

🌩️

Storm Surge Overflow

When hail hits, your phone rings off the hook. You physically can't respond to 50 leads at once. The ones you don't reach in the first hour end up signing with the roofer who called them back first.

👻

Estimate Ghosting

You spend an hour on a roof inspection, put together a detailed proposal, and then hear nothing. Without a structured follow-up system, most estimates die in the homeowner's inbox while they shop around.

🏃

Competition Wins on Speed

Larger roofing franchises have full call centers. When a lead comes in from Google, they call within minutes. If you're on a job, you call back hours later — and the job is already gone.

How NovaOps Helps

Automated speed-to-lead that wins more roofs

⚡

Instant Lead Follow-Up

Every new lead — from Google Ads, Local Services, website forms, or calls — receives an automated text and email within 5 minutes. You're always first, regardless of how busy your crew is. Clients see 3x more closed jobs from the same lead volume.

📬

Multi-Touch Estimate Sequence

After every inspection, an automated follow-up sequence runs for 14 days — text, email, and voicemail drop. Homeowners are reminded, educated, and moved toward a decision. Average estimate close rate improves by 60–80%.

📞

AI Phone Handling

Our AI answers overflow calls during storm surges, collects address and damage info, and routes urgent jobs to your crew. You never miss a storm lead again, even when you're fielding 30 calls in an afternoon.
