HVAC Company Doubled Bookings During Peak Season

Arctic Air HVAC was drowning in missed calls during their busiest months. After deploying AI automation, they answered every lead, doubled their bookings, and added $22K in extra revenue — with zero new staff.

The Problem

Peak Season Was Leaking Revenue

📵

Missed Calls During Peak Season

When the heat hit, phones rang non-stop. Technicians were in the field, the office was swamped, and dozens of potential jobs went unanswered — straight to a competitor.

🗓️

Slow Scheduling Process

Every booking required a back-and-forth phone call to check technician availability. The process was slow, error-prone, and consumed hours of office staff time each day.

🔄

No Maintenance Renewal System

Hundreds of past customers had no reminder system to re-sign their annual maintenance agreements. Revenue that should have been recurring was being left on the table.

What We Built

Every Lead Captured. Every Job Booked.

Step 01

Missed Call Textback

Within 60 seconds of any missed call, an automated text went out with a booking link. No lead slipped away — every caller got an instant response even when no one could answer.

Step 02

AI Appointment Booking

A conversational AI handled scheduling entirely via text — checking availability, confirming job type, and booking the appointment without any staff involvement.

Step 03

Maintenance Renewal Sequences

Annual maintenance customers received automated renewal reminders 60, 30, and 7 days before expiry — turning a one-time service into reliable recurring revenue.

The Outcome

Peak Season Became Their Best Quarter Ever
