The Reality

Service businesses miss nearly a third of all inbound calls. On the job, in a meeting, driving, after hours — it happens constantly. And every missed call isn't just a lost call. It's a lost customer who's actively looking for what you sell, had their credit card ready, and went to someone else because you weren't there.

The fix isn't hiring a receptionist. It's an automated text-back that fires the instant a call goes to voicemail — so you stay in the conversation before they move on.

How It Works

From missed call to
booked appointment in minutes.

1

Call Goes to Voicemail

You miss the call. It happens. Doesn't matter anymore — because what happens next changes everything.

2

Instant Text-Back (Under 60 Seconds)

Within 60 seconds, the caller receives a personalized text: "Hey [name], sorry I missed you! I'm [your name] from [business]. What can I help you with today?" Simple, human, instant.

3

AI Qualifies the Lead

If they respond, AI handles the conversation — understands their need, asks your qualification questions, and collects the information you need. All over text, at their convenience.

4

Booking or Hot Lead Alert

AI books the appointment directly, or — if they're a hot prospect who needs a call — notifies you with full context so you can jump in at the right moment. Either way, the lead is captured.
