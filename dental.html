The Challenges

Four silent revenue killers in your practice

🪑

Empty Appointment Slots

Last-minute cancellations leave your hygienists and chair time sitting idle. Without a real-time fill system, those slots almost always stay empty. Each open hygiene appointment represents $150–$250 in lost production — before the dentist even sits down.

Avg. 8–12 open slots/week for a 3-chair practice
😴

Lapsed Patient Reactivation

Most practices have hundreds of patients who haven't been seen in 18+ months. They didn't leave upset — life just got busy. A single targeted reactivation campaign can recover $15,000–$40,000 in dormant production in 30 days.

Avg. 340 lapsed patients per 1,000-patient practice
📞

Manual Reminder Calls

Your front desk spends 60–90 minutes per day making reminder calls that often go to voicemail. This is the lowest-value use of their time — and when they miss calling, no-show rates spike on those days.

90 mins/day wasted on outbound reminder calls
🌐

Weak Online Reputation

Dental is one of the most review-driven service categories. A practice with 40 reviews loses to a competitor with 400. Most practices collect reviews only when a patient spontaneously leaves one — which is rare without a prompt.

68% of patients choose dentist based on reviews
How It Works

A complete patient automation system

We build five automation loops that run 24/7 in the background — each one targeting a specific revenue gap in your practice.

Works with Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, and Curve
HIPAA-compliant messaging via encrypted SMS + email

01

New Patient Lead Capture

Every inquiry from Google, Facebook, or your website is responded to within 90 seconds. The AI collects insurance info, preferred appointment times, and routes them directly to booking.

02

Smart Appointment Reminders

Multi-step reminders sent 72 hrs, 24 hrs, and 2 hrs before each appointment. Patients confirm or reschedule with one tap, giving you time to backfill any opening.

03

Last-Minute Cancellation Fill

When a cancellation comes in, an automated waitlist message goes out to 3–5 nearby patients, often filling the slot within 30 minutes.

04

Lapsed Patient Reactivation

Patients overdue for hygiene automatically receive a warm 3-message sequence inviting them back — with a one-click booking link. No staff involvement required.

05

Post-Visit Review Request

Two hours after an appointment, patients receive a personalized message asking about their experience. Positive responders are guided to leave a Google review. Concerns go directly to the doctor.
