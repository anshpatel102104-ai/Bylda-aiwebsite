FAQ

Common Questions

What's the difference between Launchpad and Nova?
Launchpad is for the build phase — 10 AI tools to validate ideas, create pitches, map go-to-market, find customers, and generate content. Nova is for the operate phase — done-for-you CRM, lead capture, automations, follow-up, onboarding, and reporting systems. Launch plan unlocks Launchpad. Operate plan adds Nova.
Can I start on Free and upgrade later?
Yes. The Starter plan is permanently free with access to core validation tools. You can upgrade to Launch, Operate, or Scale at any time — your data and history carry over automatically.
How long does Nova OS setup take?
Typically 14 days from your onboarding call. We handle everything — you complete a 60-minute kickoff session, then we build and configure all six Nova systems for your specific business.
Do I need both Launchpad and Nova?
Not necessarily. Pre-launch founders often start with Launchpad only (Launch plan). Established businesses that already have a product skip straight to Nova (Operate plan). The Scale plan is for businesses ready to optimize and expand aggressively.
Is there a contract or commitment?
No long-term contracts. All plans are month-to-month and can be cancelled any time. The Scale plan has a 30-day notice period (not a minimum term) because of the custom build work involved — you can still cancel, we just ask for 30 days to wind down cleanly.
What does it actually cost all-in, including platform fees?
Your LaunchpadNova plan price covers all AI tool usage and Nova OS access. The only additional costs are third-party platforms: GoHighLevel runs $97–$297/month depending on your tier, and Twilio SMS typically costs $20–$80/month based on your lead volume. We help you set up both at the right tier on your kickoff call — no surprises on your bill. Total all-in for the Operate plan is typically $270–$380/month.
Is the 14-day setup guarantee actually enforceable?
Yes — it's in the service agreement, not just a headline. If your automations aren't fully built, tested, and live within 14 days of your kickoff call, we continue working at no additional charge until they are. The only way this guarantee doesn't apply is if you haven't completed your onboarding steps (access grants, calendar integration, etc.) — we can't build what we can't access. We've never missed a launch date when clients complete their side on time.
What if I sign up and it doesn't generate ROI for my business?
For Operate and Scale plans with Nova OS: if the system isn't booking appointments after launch, we keep optimizing at no charge. If you're still unsatisfied after 30 days of live operation, we'll refund your subscription — no questions asked. We make money when you see results, so it's in our interest to make sure the system works for your specific business before you're billed again.
