The insight-to-action gap

The first generation of conversation intelligence did something genuinely new: it made sales calls searchable. That is now a commodity — the underlying models are available to anyone and accuracy differences between vendors have collapsed to rounding errors.

What did not get solved is what happens next. A platform flags a signal. Now someone has to notice it, decide it matters, open the deal record, update the field, adjust the strategy, and tell their manager. Multiply those completion rates together and you get the value actually delivered, which is a fraction of the demo.

FAQ

Conversation intelligence, answered.

Conversation intelligence is software that records, transcribes, and analyses customer conversations to surface what happened — buying signals, objections, competitor mentions, talk ratios, and coaching moments. Bylda goes a step further and executes on what it finds, writing the CRM and drafting the follow-up.

Traditional conversation intelligence produces insight and hands it back to a human to act on. Every step between insight and action has a completion rate below one, so most of the promised value evaporates. Bylda closes that gap: it writes the fields, drafts the message, and updates the forecast automatically.
