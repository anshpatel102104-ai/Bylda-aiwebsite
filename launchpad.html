COMMON QUESTIONS

Launchpad FAQ

How is Launchpad different from ChatGPT for business planning? +

ChatGPT requires you to know what to ask and how to interpret answers. Launchpad's 10 tools are purpose-built for each specific founder task — idea validation, investor pitch structure, GTM planning — with frameworks and outputs optimized for real business decisions, not general conversation.

Do I need a business idea before I start, or can Launchpad help me find one? +

You need at least a rough concept. Launchpad's Idea Validator and Idea vs Idea tools help you evaluate and compare concepts — but you bring the starting point. They stress-test and sharpen what you already have.

Can I use Launchpad if I already have a launched business? +

Yes — especially for refining your pitch, mapping a new GTM channel, or generating investor outreach. But if you're already operational, you'll likely get more leverage from Nova OS, which automates your CRM, follow-ups, and client onboarding. Many founders use both in parallel.

How long does it take to go through all 10 tools? +

Most founders complete the full validation and launch workflow in a single weekend. Each tool takes 5–20 minutes.
