The Problem

Most leads need 7+ touches.
You're giving them one.

A lead opts in, gets a welcome email, and then hears nothing. Or worse — gets blasted with irrelevant bulk email until they unsubscribe. Neither converts. The businesses winning with email treat every lead as an individual — sending the right message at the right moment based on what they've done, not just when they signed up. That's what AI email automation makes possible at scale.

Features

The full email engine.
Built for conversion.

👋

Welcome Sequences

First impressions are everything. Custom welcome sequences introduce your brand, set expectations, and guide new leads toward their first conversion — starting the moment they opt in.

💧

Nurture Drip Campaigns

Multi-week nurture sequences that educate, build trust, and position your offer — so when a lead is ready to buy, you're the obvious choice. Up to 90 days of automated touchpoints.

⚡

Behavioral Triggers

Opened but didn't click? Visited your pricing page twice? AI detects intent signals and triggers the right follow-up automatically — while the lead is still hot.

🧪

A/B Testing

Subject lines, send times, CTAs, and body copy are continuously tested. Winners deploy automatically. Your sequences get smarter every week without manual intervention.

🎯

Smart Segmentation

Leads are automatically sorted by source, interest, behavior, and stage in the funnel. Each segment gets messaging built for where they are — not a one-size-fits-all blast.

📬

Deliverability Optimization

Domain warm-up, SPF/DKIM/DMARC configuration, list hygiene, and sending reputation management — so your emails land in the inbox, not spam. High deliverability from day one.
