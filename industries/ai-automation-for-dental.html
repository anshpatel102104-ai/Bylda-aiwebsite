FAQ

Questions from
dental practices.

How does AI handle recall reminders for dental patients? +

The system tracks each patient's last hygiene visit and automatically triggers a recall campaign when they're approaching or past their 6-month mark. Messages are sent via SMS (and email as backup), and when a patient replies to book, AI handles scheduling and adds them directly to your calendar. No staff involvement needed until the appointment is confirmed.

Can the system fill last-minute cancellations automatically? +

Yes. When a cancellation is detected in your calendar, the system immediately broadcasts the open slot to your waitlist via text. Patients reply to claim the appointment — the first confirmed reply gets the slot. Your front desk doesn't need to make a single call. Most open slots are filled within minutes.

Does this integrate with Dentrix, Eaglesoft, or Curve? +

NovaOps AI is designed to work alongside your existing practice management software. We integrate with Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Curve Dental, and other leading PMS platforms via API or data sync. You keep using the software your team already knows — we layer automation on top of it.

Is patient data secure and HIPAA compliant? +

Yes. All data handling, messaging infrastructure, and storage meets HIPAA requirements. We sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every dental practice. Patient information is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is restricted, and audit logs are maintained. Your practice and your patients are fully protected.

What does 14-day setup actually involve? +

After a kickoff call, our team handles everything: connecting to your PMS, building out recall and confirmation sequences, setting up the cancellation fill system, configuring your waitlist, and testing every flow before anything goes live. Your team reviews and approves the messaging. Most practices are fully live in 10-14 days with zero disruption to their daily operations.
