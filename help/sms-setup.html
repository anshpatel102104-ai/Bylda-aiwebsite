SMS automation setup guide

From provisioning your number to staying compliant with A2P 10DLC — everything you need to send SMS that actually gets delivered.

A2P 10DLCComplianceOpt-in / Opt-outDeliverability

Step 1: Phone number provisioning

You have three options for your SMS sending number. Each has tradeoffs:

New Local Number (Recommended)

Best choice

We provision a local area-code number that matches your market. Local numbers get higher response rates because leads recognize the area code. NovaOps handles this for you during onboarding.

Port your existing business number

You can transfer an existing number to NovaOps. Porting takes 7–14 business days depending on your current carrier. Provide your account info and PIN from your current carrier to start the process.

Toll-free number (1-800)

Toll-free numbers have a separate verification path (TF verification) and can send higher volumes. Good for established brands, but slightly lower response rates than local numbers for cold outreach.

Step 2: A2P 10DLC registration

⚠️ Required by all US carriers: Since 2022, all businesses sending application-to-person (A2P) SMS to US numbers must be registered through The Campaign Registry (TCR). Unregistered numbers are filtered or blocked by carriers. NovaOps handles this registration for you.

10DLC ("10-Digit Long Code") registration requires two things: Brand Registration and Campaign Registration. Here's what we need from you:

Information required for Brand Registration

  • ✓ Legal business name (must match IRS records)
  • ✓ EIN (Employer Identification Number)
  • ✓ Business address (must be a real US address)
  • ✓ Business website URL
  • ✓ Vertical / business type (real estate, health, home services, etc.)

Campaign registration details

A Campaign describes how you'll use SMS. You'll need to provide:

  • ✓ Use case description (e.g., "Lead follow-up and appointment reminders for roofing company")
  • ✓ Sample message (2–3 example SMS messages you'll send)
  • ✓ Opt-in method (how contacts consented to receive messages)

Processing time: Brand registration typically completes within 24 hours. Campaign registration takes 3–7 business days for approval from TCR. During this window, your SMS messages may be filtered by some carriers — this is normal and resolves once approved.

Step 3: Opt-in and opt-out flows

TCPA law requires that you have documented consent before sending marketing SMS. NovaOps handles opt-in and opt-out automatically — but you must make sure consent is collected at the point of lead capture.

Website form opt-in language (required)

By submitting this form, you consent to receive text messages from [Business Name] at the number provided. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. View our Privacy Policy at [URL].

Auto opt-out handling

NovaOps automatically recognizes opt-out keywords and stops messaging the contact. Recognized opt-out keywords:

STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT

When a contact sends any of these keywords, NovaOps: (1) stops all automated messages to that number, (2) marks the contact as "SMS Opted Out" in your CRM, and (3) sends a final confirmation: "You've been unsubscribed from [Business Name] text messages. No more messages will be sent."

Message limits and best send times

10DLC throughput limits

Standard 10DLC: ~75 messages/minute per number. Our plans include message pooling across multiple numbers for high-volume businesses. If you send more than 5,000 messages/day, contact us to set up a number pool.

Character limits

Standard SMS: 160 characters. Messages longer than 160 characters are split into segments (each billed as 1 SMS). Unicode characters (emojis) reduce the limit to 70 chars per segment. Keep follow-up texts under 160 chars when possible.
