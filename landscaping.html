The Challenges

Why landscaping businesses stay stuck in feast-or-famine

❄️

Seasonal Revenue Gaps

Spring is slammed, summer is solid, but fall and winter leave you scrambling for work. Without proactive off-season outreach and retention systems, you re-acquire the same customers every spring instead of keeping them on retainer.

🔂

One-Time vs. Recurring

Most customers who call for a one-time cleanup or mulch job would happily pay for ongoing maintenance — they just never get asked in a timely, compelling way. This upgrade opportunity is worth 5–10x the initial job value.

📋

Quote Follow-Up Lag

You send a quote for a landscape design or seasonal contract and then get busy with existing jobs. Three days pass. The customer picked the company that followed up the same afternoon. Speed and persistence win landscaping deals.

How NovaOps Helps

Build a landscaping business that runs year-round

🌱

Seasonal Reactivation

At the start of spring and fall, automated campaigns reach out to past customers with seasonal service offers. Re-engaging a past customer costs 5x less than acquiring a new one. Our clients see 2x more recurring clients by spring of their first full year.

📬

Quote Follow-Up Sequences

Every quote triggers a 7-day follow-up sequence — a reminder at day 2, a value-add message at day 4, and a soft close at day 7. Quote acceptance rates improve to 55% on average from a typical industry rate of 25–30%.

⭐

Review Automation

After every completed service, customers receive a friendly review request. Landscaping relies heavily on neighborhood word-of-mouth and Google visibility. Our clients collect 3x more reviews than before automation, dominating local search results.
