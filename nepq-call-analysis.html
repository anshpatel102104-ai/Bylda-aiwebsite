What is NEPQ call analysis?

NEPQ call analysis checks whether sales conversations follow the Neuro-Emotional Persuasion Questioning flow — connecting, situation, problem-awareness, solution-awareness, consequence and commitment questions — and where reps skip stages or start presenting too early.

What Bylda tracks

On every call, automatically.

Observable conduct only — each finding links to the moment in the recording, so anyone can check it.

  • Which question stages appeared, in what order
  • How long the rep stayed in problem-awareness before presenting
  • Whether consequence questions were asked at all
  • Rep talk time after the buyer admits a problem
  • Whether the call closed on a commitment question

Where it breaks down

Three patterns worth catching early.

Pitching at the first admission

The buyer names a problem and the rep starts presenting immediately.

Consequence skipped

The cost of doing nothing is never explored, so urgency never forms.

Commitment never asked

The call ends on interest rather than a decision.

FAQ

NEPQ call analysis, answered.

What is NEPQ in sales?

Neuro-Emotional Persuasion Questioning, a question-led selling approach where the rep guides the buyer to articulate their own problem, its consequences and the change they want before presenting a solution.

What’s the most common NEPQ mistake on calls?

Moving to the pitch as soon as the buyer admits a problem, skipping consequence questions that build urgency.

Can software tell which question stage a rep is in?

It can classify questions by purpose and order with reasonable reliability, and every classification links to the timestamp so it can be checked.

Is NEPQ better than other methodologies?

That depends on your market. The useful question is whether your team executes the method you chose — and whether doing so is associated with better outcomes in your own deals.
