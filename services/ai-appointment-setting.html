FAQ

Questions we get asked
before people start.

How quickly can we go live?
Most clients are fully live within 14 days. The onboarding process includes a strategy call (day 1–2), script and sequence buildout (day 3–7), integration and testing (day 8–12), and a live QA review before we flip the switch. Complex setups may take up to 21 days, but we move fast.
Can I customize the qualification scripts?
Yes — scripts are fully custom to your industry, your offer, and your buyer criteria. We start with an intake call to understand exactly who your ideal appointment is and what makes someone qualified. You review and approve every script before it goes live. Changes can be made at any time.
What happens to leads that don't qualify?
Unqualified leads don't get deleted — they enter a long-term nurture sequence. The AI will re-engage them at intervals (30, 60, 90 days) and re-qualify them when their situation may have changed. Many clients recover significant revenue from leads that initially didn't qualify but came back months later.
Does it work for my industry?
We have pre-built playbooks for Real Estate, Med Spa, Insurance, Solar, Roofing, HVAC, Dental, and Chiropractic. For industries outside this list, we build from scratch using your knowledge of your buyer. If your business runs on scheduled appointments, this system works for you.
What CRMs do you integrate with?
We integrate with GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, and most other major CRMs via native integration or Zapier/Make webhooks. Calendar integrations include Calendly, Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, and Cal.com. If you use a CRM not listed, ask us — we can almost certainly connect it.
What is the 14-day live guarantee?
We guarantee your system is live and actively booking appointments within 14 days of onboarding — or your first month is free. This is not a soft commitment. We've never missed this window for a client who completed their onboarding steps on time, but we stand behind it regardless.
