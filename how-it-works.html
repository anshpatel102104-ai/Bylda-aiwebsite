FAQ

Common questions about how it works

Bylda connects to your calendar once and joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams calls silently — no bot announcement, no rep action required. It captures audio, transcript, and email context in real time. Every subsequent call is covered automatically from the moment you connect.

Bylda's CRM automation supports Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and other major platforms. It maps conversational data to both standard and custom fields in your schema — so your pipeline stays accurate without reps entering a single note. See all supported integrations.

Most conversation intelligence tools record and summarize — then leave the rep to act on it. Bylda extracts structured data from every call (stage changes, decision-maker detection, objection classification, risk signals) and writes it directly to your CRM. The output is an accurate, updated pipeline — not a transcript sitting in a dashboard. Learn more on our Bylda vs Gong page.

Each night, Bylda re-scores your entire pipeline, flags deals that have gone quiet, drafts follow-up messages written in each rep's voice, and assembles a ranked action list. It lands before your first meeting — the highest-impact deal, the highest-risk opportunity, and three ready-to-send messages, prepared automatically.

Most teams are live within one business day. Bylda connects to your calendar, conferencing tools, and CRM through guided OAuth flows — no engineering required. Your first automated CRM update arrives after your first call. Pilots typically run two weeks on one pod, on your real pipeline.
