How a Dallas Roofing Company Tripled Revenue in 90 Days

A multi-location roofing company in Dallas, TX was missing nearly half their storm-season leads. With NovaOps AI, they went from 8 to 26 closed jobs per month — and added $48k in revenue in their very first month.

Multi-location Roofing Company Dallas, TX $4.2M Annual Revenue 90-Day Transformation
The Problem

Storm Season Was Overwhelming Their Team

During peak storm season, lead volume would spike dramatically — but the team had no system to handle the surge. The result: missed opportunities and revenue left on the table.

📞
Missing 40% of storm-season leads. When hail storms hit Dallas, inbound calls and form fills would spike overnight. The small office team simply couldn't answer every call, and with no after-hours coverage, nearly half of all leads went unanswered — most of them going to a competitor who picked up first.
⏳
Estimate follow-up taking 2–3 days. After a site visit, sending out a quote involved manual data entry, drafting emails by hand, and chasing down approvals. By the time a homeowner received their estimate, they'd already signed with a faster-moving company. The average delay was 48–72 hours.
🔨
Crews too busy to call leads back. The business owner and lead estimator were both on rooftops and job sites during the day. There was no dedicated sales person, so new lead callbacks happened sporadically — sometimes days later. Warm leads went completely cold.
The Solution

A Fully Automated Lead-to-Close System

NovaOps AI deployed a four-system stack that handled every lead from the moment they called or filled out a form — to the booked estimate — without requiring the owner or crew to lift a finger.

✓
AI Instant Response System — Every inbound call, web form, or Facebook ad lead triggers an immediate AI-powered SMS and email within 60 seconds, 24/7, including nights and weekends during storm surges.
✓
Lead Qualification Flow — The AI asks the right questions: damage type, roof age, homeowner vs. renter, insurance carrier. Qualified leads are automatically pushed to the estimate queue with full context.
✓
Estimate Follow-Up Sequence — After a site visit, the system automatically sends the quote PDF, follows up at 24h and 48h, handles objections via SMS, and prompts a calendar booking for contract signing.
✓
Missed Call Text-Back — Any call that goes to voicemail triggers an immediate SMS: "Hey, we just missed your call — we have crews in your area this week. Want to schedule a free roof inspection?" Response rate: 38%.
