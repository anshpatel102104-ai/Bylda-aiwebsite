Bylda reads the most sensitive thing your company owns: what customers and reps actually say to each other. We built the security posture that responsibility demands, and we publish it plainly — including the part about your own team.

Encrypted end to end

Conversations, transcripts and derived data are encrypted in transit and at rest.

Access that mirrors your CRM

If someone can’t see an opportunity in your CRM, they can’t see its conversations in Bylda. Single sign-on requirements are scoped during evaluation.

Consent-aware recording

Bylda works from recordings your team already captures under your consent settings, and honours exclusion and deletion requests.

Full audit trail

No training on your data

Your conversations are never used to train shared or third-party models. Contractually, not just as policy.

The line we don't cross

Isolation is the default.

Every customer's data lives in a logically isolated tenant with its own encryption context. Models read your data to serve you, in your session, and nothing crosses that boundary.

  • No shared embeddings between tenants
  • No human review of your transcripts without written, per-incident consent
  • Subprocessors published, with 30 days' notice before any change
  • Deletion honored within 30 days, backups included

The commitment about your own team

Most security pages stop at customer data. Bylda analyses how your employees work, so there is a second set of commitments that belongs here.

  • Reps see their own audit first. A rep is never in a position where their manager has read a finding about them that they have not seen.
  • Findings describe conduct, not character. Bylda reports what was said and done, with the timestamp. It does not rate people, score personality, or infer emotional state.
  • No leaderboards. Ranking reps against each other produces gamed behavior and worse data, which defeats the purpose of measuring at all.
  • Consent is enforced per region. In two-party consent jurisdictions recording does not begin until consent is captured. Administrators configure this once and it applies to everyone.
  • Deletion is real. Remove a conversation and the derived traces, signals and audit findings go with it.

This is not only an ethics position. A workforce-analytics deployment that a sales team resents gets worked around, and worked-around measurement is worthless. See what a rep actually sees.
