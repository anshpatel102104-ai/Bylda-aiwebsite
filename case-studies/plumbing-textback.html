Case Study

Plumber's Missed Calls Generated $18K in Month One

Tony V. had no idea how many emergency calls he was missing. After deploying AI textback and automated booking, he recovered $18K in the first month from jobs that would have gone straight to a competitor.

Client: FastFlow Plumbing · Tony V. · Emergency & Residential Plumbing

The Problem

Every Missed Call Was a Lost Job

🚨

Missing Emergency Calls

Plumbing emergencies don't wait. When Tony was under a sink or driving between jobs, calls rang through to voicemail — and customers called the next plumber immediately.

🏃

Losing Jobs to Faster Competitors

Larger plumbing companies had call centers open 24/7. A one-person shop couldn't compete on response speed — until automation leveled the playing field.

⭐

No Review System

Tony did great work but never asked for reviews. His Google profile sat at 3.8 stars with only 12 reviews, making it harder for new customers to choose him over rated competitors.

What We Built

60-Second Response. Every Single Call.

Step 01

60-Second Textback

Every missed call triggered an instant text within 60 seconds: "Hey, this is Tony's Plumbing — sorry we missed you! We're often on job sites. Can I help you book a time?" No lead escaped.

Step 02

AI Booking Bot

The follow-up text included a booking link. For urgent calls, an AI chat assistant collected job details, confirmed availability, and scheduled the appointment — 24/7, no phone required.

Step 03

Review Automation Post-Service

Two hours after every completed job, an automated text went out asking for a review. Happy customers were sent directly to Google. Any issues were captured privately before going public.

The Outcome

$18K in Month One. 60+ Reviews. 4.9 Stars.
