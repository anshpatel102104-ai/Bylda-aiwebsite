How Launchpad Works — Complete Walkthrough for Founders

Ansh PatelMay 20256 min read

Launchpad is a suite of 20 AI-powered tools built specifically for founders — from the idea stage through funding readiness. This walkthrough covers every tool, how to use it, and who gets the most value from each one.

The 20 Tools, One by One

1. Idea Validator

Input your business idea in plain language. The Idea Validator scores it across five dimensions: market size, competition level, monetization clarity, timing, and founder-market fit. You get a score, a risk breakdown, and specific questions to investigate before moving forward. Best for: founders with 2-5 ideas who need a structured way to compare them.

2. Kill My Idea

The adversarial counterpart to the Idea Validator. Where most tools flatter you, Kill My Idea argues against your concept — surfacing the objections investors will raise, the assumptions you haven't tested, and the market realities that could sink you. Use it after the Idea Validator to stress-test whatever scored highest.

3. Persona Builder

Generates detailed ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) personas based on your product and market inputs. Outputs include demographics, psychographics, pain points, buying triggers, objections, and preferred channels. Much more actionable than generic marketing personas.

4. GTM Strategy Builder

Takes your product, ICP, and stage as inputs and produces a full go-to-market plan with channel recommendations, 30/60/90-day milestones, and specific tactics. One of the highest-leverage tools in the suite for founders preparing to launch.

5. Business Plan Generator

Creates a structured business plan document covering executive summary, market analysis, competitive landscape, operations, and financial projections framework. The output is formatted for investors and lenders — not just your own planning.

6. Competitor Scanner

Input your space and the tool maps your competitive landscape: key players, their positioning, pricing models, strengths, weaknesses, and gaps you can exploit. Saves 3-4 hours of manual research per competitor.

7. Pitch Deck Generator

Generates slide-by-slide content for a 10-slide investor pitch: problem, solution, market size, traction, team, business model, go-to-market, competition, financials, and ask. Output includes talking points for each slide.

8. Funding Readiness Scorer

Evaluates your startup across the 10 dimensions investors assess most heavily. Gives you a score per category and a prioritized gap-closure roadmap so you know exactly what to fix before approaching investors.

9. Ad Copy Generator

Produces platform-specific ad copy for Meta, Google, and LinkedIn from your offer, audience, and angle inputs. Generates five variations per platform so you can A/B test immediately.

10. Email Sequence Builder

Builds complete nurture and sales email sequences (5-7 emails) with subject lines, preview text, and body copy. Optimized for your ICP and desired outcome — whether that's booking a call, trial signup, or direct purchase.

11. Landing Page Copy Generator

Outputs full landing page copy: hero headline and subhead, three benefit sections, social proof framework, FAQ, and CTA copy. Based on proven conversion copywriting frameworks.

12. KPI Dashboard Builder

Generates a custom KPI framework for your business model and stage. Tells you which metrics to track, how to calculate them, what good benchmarks look like, and which tools to use for measurement.

13. SEO Content Planner

Produces a keyword strategy and content calendar for your niche — including primary keywords, content clusters, and headline ideas for 3 months of blog content. Built around topical authority principles.

14. Investor Outreach Templates

Cold email templates for reaching out to angel investors and VCs, with personalization prompts for each. Includes follow-up sequences and guidance on what to include and exclude.

Free vs. Paid Tier

The free tier gives you access to the Idea Validator, Kill My Idea, and the Ad Copy Generator with a limited number of monthly runs. The paid tier (see pricing) unlocks all 20 tools with unlimited runs, priority processing, and the ability to save and export your outputs.

Getting Started in 5 Steps

  • Step 1: Create your free account at launchpad-bylda
  • Step 2: Run your idea through the Idea Validator — write one clear sentence describing your concept
  • Step 3: Run the same idea through Kill My Idea and read the objections seriously
  • Step 4: If the idea survives, build your ICP with the Persona Builder
  • Step 5: Use the GTM Strategy Builder to create your launch roadmap

Who Gets the Most Value

Launchpad is most powerful for founders in the pre-revenue to early-revenue stage who are moving fast and need structured outputs — not blank canvases. If you're deciding between ideas, preparing to pitch investors, or building your first marketing materials, these tools compress weeks of work into hours. Enterprise founders with dedicated teams will find it more useful as a first-draft and ideation tool than as a primary research source.
