AI not responding to new leads

"A new lead came in but the AI never sent a message. The contact is in my CRM but no follow-up happened."

  • 1Check your webhook is active. Go to Integrations → Webhooks and confirm the inbound webhook status shows active. If it shows inactive or error, click "Reactivate."
  • 2Verify the lead has a phone number or email. The AI requires at least one contact method. Open the lead in your CRM and check the phone field. Empty phone numbers are the #1 cause of silent failures.
  • 3Check if the lead was created during a paused sequence window. If you have "business hours only" enabled, the AI queues after-hours leads and sends the next morning. Check your Automation Logs for a "queued" status.
  • 4Confirm the lead source is mapped to a sequence. Go to Automation → Lead Source Mapping. If the lead's source is "Unknown" or unmapped, no sequence fires. Add a default sequence for unknown sources or a catch-all rule.
  • 5Check the lead isn't opted-out. If the phone number previously texted STOP to any NovaOps number, they're blocked. Check Contacts → Opt-Out List. You'll need the contact to re-opt-in before messaging can resume.

CRM not syncing / contacts not updating

"Leads that NovaOps has been talking to aren't showing updated pipeline stages, conversation notes, or booked appointments in my CRM."

  • 1Re-authorize your CRM connection. Tokens expire after 90 days for some CRMs. Go to Integrations → [Your CRM] → click "Reconnect." Re-authorize with admin credentials. This fixes 70% of sync issues.
  • 2Check your pipeline stage mapping. If you recently renamed stages in your CRM, NovaOps is writing to old stage IDs that no longer exist. Go to Integrations → Stage Mapping and re-map each stage.
  • 3Verify your CRM API rate limits. If you have a high lead volume, your CRM API may be throttling requests. Check the CRM error logs in Integrations → Activity Log. Look for 429 Too Many Requests errors. Contact support to enable request queuing.
  • 4Confirm the CRM contact exists before the update is attempted. NovaOps matches contacts by phone or email. If the phone number in NovaOps has a different format than in your CRM (e.g., +15551234567 vs 555-123-4567), the match fails. Enable "Fuzzy Match" in Integrations → Settings.

Missed calls not triggering voice AI

"Someone called my business number, it rang and went to voicemail — but the voice AI didn't pick up and no lead was created."

  • 1Verify your number is forwarded to NovaOps. Voice AI only works if calls route through the NovaOps system. Check Integrations → Voice → Number Configuration. Your business number should show "Forwarded to NovaOps" with a green status dot.
  • 2Check your ring time setting. If your ring-before-pickup is set to "0" (immediate), but calls are still going to your carrier voicemail, your carrier's voicemail is intercepting before NovaOps. Contact your carrier to disable their voicemail service — NovaOps handles this function.
  • 3Confirm voice AI is enabled on your plan. Voice AI is available on Growth plan and above. If you're on Starter, upgrade to Growth or contact us about adding voice as an add-on.
  • 4Test with a direct call to your NovaOps-provisioned number (not your forwarded number). If voice AI answers when calling the NovaOps number but not your main number, the issue is in call forwarding, not the AI setup.

Appointments not booking

"The AI is having a conversation and qualifying the lead, but when it tries to book an appointment, it either fails silently or the lead says they never got a confirmation."

  • 1Recheck your calendar authorization. Calendar tokens expire. Go to Appointments → Calendar → click "Test Connection." If it fails, click "Reconnect" and re-authorize with your calendar provider (Google, Outlook, etc.).
  • 2Check for availability gaps. If your calendar has no available slots in the next 14 days (your booking window), the AI can't offer times. Open your calendar and verify there are open slots during your configured working hours.
  • 3Confirm your booking window isn't too restrictive. If minimum notice is set to 48 hours and your only available slot is tomorrow, leads can't book it. Reduce minimum notice or add slots further out.
  • 4Check confirmation message delivery. If appointments book but leads say they didn't get confirmation, check your SMS/email sending status in Automation Logs → Appointment Confirmations. A failed delivery here means your SMS number may need re-verification or your email sender domain needs DNS setup.

SMS not delivering / low delivery rate

"Messages show as 'sent' in NovaOps but leads aren't receiving them, or my delivery rate has dropped below 80%."

  • 1Check your A2P 10DLC registration status. Go to Integrations → SMS → Registration Status. If your campaign shows "Pending" or "Rejected," messages to major carriers (AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon) will be filtered. See our SMS setup guide for registration details.
  • 2Review your message content for spam triggers. Messages with excessive capitalization, URLs that aren't your registered domain, prize/lottery language, or too many special characters are filtered by carriers. Remove these from your templates.
  • 3Check if you're hitting quiet hours. TCPA prohibits SMS before 8 AM or after 9 PM in the recipient's time zone. Leads in a different time zone than yours may be hit by quiet hours enforcement. Enable "Auto Timezone Detection" in SMS Settings.
  • 4Review the error codes in your delivery logs. Go to Automation → Logs → SMS Delivery. Common codes: 30003 = unreachable number, 30006 = landline (can't receive SMS), 30008 = unknown error (usually carrier filtering). High volumes of 30008 mean your campaign registration needs updating.

Duplicate contacts in CRM

"The same lead appears multiple times in my CRM — once from the original form submission and once from NovaOps creating a new contact."

  • 1Enable deduplication in NovaOps. Go to Integrations → CRM → Settings → enable "Deduplication." Set the match key (email is most reliable; phone as backup). NovaOps will look up existing contacts before creating new ones.
  • 2Ensure phone numbers are in E.164 format consistently. If your CRM stores 555-123-4567 but NovaOps sends +15551234567, dedup matching fails. Enable "Phone Normalization" in integration settings.
  • 3For existing duplicates: use your CRM's native deduplication tool to merge existing duplicate contacts. In GHL this is Contacts → Merge Contacts. In HubSpot it's Contacts → Manage Duplicates.
🔀

Lead assigned to wrong follow-up sequence

"A Facebook ad lead received the same generic message as an organic website lead. The sequence didn't match the source."

  • 1Check your lead source field is being passed in the webhook payload. Open Automation → Logs → find the lead in question → check the raw payload. If lead_source is empty or "null", your form or ad platform isn't passing this field.
  • 2Verify source mapping in Automation → Lead Source Mapping. Confirm that "Facebook Ads" (or however your platform labels it) is mapped to your Facebook-specific sequence. Check spelling — "facebook_ads" and "Facebook Ads" are treated as different values.
  • 3Add a fallback sequence for any unmapped sources. In Lead Source Mapping → Default Sequence → assign your general follow-up sequence. This prevents leads from getting no messages when their source is unrecognized.
🗓️

Calendar showing unavailable times incorrectly

"The AI is telling leads there are no available times, but I have open slots in my calendar. Or it's booking during times I'm actually busy."

  • 1Check calendar sync frequency. NovaOps syncs your calendar every 15 minutes by default. If you just added availability, wait 15 minutes for the next sync, or manually trigger a sync in Appointments → Calendar → "Force Sync."
  • 2Verify which calendar is being read. If you have multiple Google calendars, NovaOps may be reading your "Birthdays" or a secondary calendar instead of your primary work calendar. Go to Appointments → Calendar → select the correct calendar in the "Read From" dropdown.
  • 3Check your time zone settings. A timezone mismatch between NovaOps (your account timezone) and your calendar (e.g., calendar set to UTC but you're in EST) causes a 5-hour offset in availability. Verify both are set to the same timezone in Account Settings and Calendar Settings.
