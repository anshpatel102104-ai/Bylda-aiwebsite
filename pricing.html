FAQ

Common Questions

What's the difference between Launchpad and Nova?
Launchpad is for the build phase — 10 AI tools to validate ideas, create pitches, map go-to-market, find customers, and generate content. Nova is for the operate phase — done-for-you CRM, lead capture, automations, follow-up, onboarding, and reporting systems. Launch plan unlocks Launchpad. Operate plan adds Nova.
Can I start on Free and upgrade later?
Yes. The Starter plan is permanently free with access to core validation tools. You can upgrade to Launch, Operate, or Scale at any time — your data and history carry over automatically.
How long does Nova OS setup take?
Typically 14 days from your onboarding call. We handle everything — you complete a 60-minute kickoff session, then we build and configure all six Nova systems for your specific business.
Do I need both Launchpad and Nova?
Not necessarily. Pre-launch founders often start with Launchpad only (Launch plan). Established businesses that already have a product skip straight to Nova (Operate plan). The Scale plan is for businesses ready to optimize and expand aggressively.
Is there a contract or commitment?
No long-term contracts. All plans are month-to-month and can be cancelled any time. Scale plan includes a 30-day minimum due to the custom build work involved.
What's the typical ROI on the Operate plan?
It depends on your lead volume and deal size, but here's what we see repeatedly: a business getting 50 leads/month and closing 20% is leaving 80% of those leads on the table after the first no-answer. Nova's automated follow-up typically recovers 15–30% of those "dead" leads. For a business with a $2k average job, that's 6–12 extra bookings per month — $12k–$24k in recovered revenue — from a $149 system. Most clients see positive ROI within the first 2–3 weeks.
What if my business isn't listed in your industries?
The automation systems work for any business that generates leads and needs follow-up — which is nearly every service business. We've built systems for law firms, gyms, dental offices, financial advisors, and more. If you're not sure whether we can help, book a free 15-minute call and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit — and what to expect.
What happens if the system doesn't perform?
Two guarantees protect you. First: if your Nova system isn't fully live within 14 days of your onboarding call, we keep building for free until it is. Second: if after 30 days you're not satisfied with the results, we refund you — no questions asked. We've put our skin in the game because we're confident the system works.
