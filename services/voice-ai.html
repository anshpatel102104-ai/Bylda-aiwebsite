How It Works

From new lead to booked appointment.

1

New lead submits a form or calls in

Any form submission, ad lead, inbound call, or landing page conversion triggers the Voice AI sequence. No manual intervention required.

2

AI voice agent calls them within 60–120 seconds

The AI calls the lead almost immediately — before they've had time to contact a competitor. The voice is natural and conversational, not robotic.

3

Natural conversation: budget, timeline, interest level

AI asks your qualification questions naturally — budget, timeline, location, specific needs. It handles objections and keeps the conversation moving toward a booking.

4

Qualified leads get booked directly into your calendar

The AI books an appointment slot in real-time during the call. A confirmation is sent via text before the lead even hangs up. No back-and-forth scheduling.

5

Unqualified leads go into a nurture sequence

If a lead isn't ready to book, they're automatically enrolled in an SMS or email nurture sequence — so you stay top of mind until they're ready.

6

You only talk to pre-qualified, booked prospects

Every appointment on your calendar has already been screened and confirmed. Your time goes to closing — not chasing or screening cold leads.

FAQ

Your Voice AI questions answered.

Does it sound robotic? +
No. Modern AI voice technology is significantly different from older robotic IVR systems. Our AI agents use natural prosody, appropriate pauses, and conversational language. Most leads don't realize they're talking to AI — and in most industries, it doesn't matter either way.
Can I customize the qualification script? +
Yes — completely. We build the qualification questions, objection handling responses, and call flow specifically for your business during onboarding. You review and approve the script before the AI ever makes a live call.
What if a lead asks something the AI doesn't know? +
The AI is trained on your business knowledge base — services, pricing, FAQs, location, and policies. If a question falls outside what it knows, it gracefully escalates: "Let me have the right person call you back about that" — and then notifies your team immediately.
How does it integrate with my calendar? +
We connect directly to Calendly, Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, and most major booking tools. The AI checks availability in real-time during the call and books the slot instantly. Confirmation texts go out automatically once the booking is made.
Is it legal to use AI for outbound calls? +
Yes, when done correctly. Our system is built to comply with FCC regulations and TCPA guidelines for outbound AI calls. We call leads who have explicitly submitted their information and opted in to be contacted — which is exactly how compliant outbound calling works. We handle the compliance setup for you.
