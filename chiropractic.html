Common Challenges

The Problems Every Chiropractic Practice Faces

📞
Missed New Patient Calls
When you're with a patient, calls go to voicemail. Most new patients move on to the next chiropractor rather than leave a message.
❌
High No-Show Rates
Manual reminder calls take time and often don't reach patients. Empty time slots are lost revenue that can't be recovered.
💸
Patients Fall Off Care Plans
Patients who miss one or two appointments often don't rebook. Without systematic follow-up, these relationships — and their treatment outcomes — go cold.
⭐
Not Enough Reviews
Happy patients forget to leave reviews. Without consistent review volume, new patients don't find you when searching for a chiropractor in your area.
