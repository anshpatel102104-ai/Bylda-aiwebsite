Setting up AI appointment booking

Connect your calendar, define your availability, and build reminder sequences that keep your show rate above 80%.

Calendar syncAvailability rulesRemindersConfirmations

Step 1: Connect your calendar

NovaOps reads your real-time availability and books directly into your calendar. No double-bookings. Supported calendar platforms:

📅

Google Calendar

Most popular. OAuth connection in 30 seconds.

🗓️

Outlook / Office 365

Enterprise and personal Microsoft accounts.

🔗

Calendly

Connect existing Calendly links to NovaOps flows.

⚡

GHL Calendar

Native integration for GoHighLevel users.

1

Authorize calendar access

In NovaOps → Appointments → Calendar Integration → click "Connect Google Calendar" (or Outlook). Authorize read/write access. NovaOps needs write access to create calendar events when appointments are booked.

2

Select the calendar to write to

If you have multiple calendars (personal, business, team), select which calendar NovaOps should add appointments to. This is typically your primary business calendar.

3

Test the connection

Click "Send Test Booking." NovaOps will create a test event in your calendar for 5 minutes from now. Check your calendar — if the event appears, your connection is working correctly. Delete the test event when confirmed.

Step 2: Define your availability rules

Availability rules tell the AI exactly when it's allowed to book appointments. Be specific — vague rules lead to awkwardly timed bookings.

Working hours

Set start/end times per day. Example: Mon–Fri 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM. You can set different hours per day (e.g., shorter hours on Friday).

Buffer time

Add buffer before and after appointments (e.g., 15 min). This prevents back-to-back bookings with no preparation time.

Minimum notice

Set how far in advance an appointment must be booked (e.g., at least 2 hours from now). Prevents last-minute surprises.

Max per day

Cap the number of AI-booked appointments per day (e.g., max 6). Prevents your calendar from being completely filled by automated bookings.

💡 Pro tip: Booking windows

Set a "booking window" — the maximum number of days ahead a lead can book (e.g., no more than 14 days out). Appointments too far in the future have higher cancellation rates.

Step 3: Customize confirmation messages

Confirmation messages are sent immediately after a lead books. A strong confirmation reduces no-shows by setting clear expectations.

SMS confirmation template

✅ You're confirmed, {{first_name}}! 📅 {{appointment_day}}, {{appointment_date}} 🕐 {{appointment_time}} {{timezone}} 📍 {{meeting_type}} — {{meeting_link_or_location}} Add to your calendar: {{calendar_add_link}} Reply CANCEL to cancel or RESCHEDULE to change your time.

Email confirmation subject line options

# Option A — Clear and simple Your appointment is confirmed — {{appointment_date}} at {{appointment_time}} # Option B — Warm and personal See you {{appointment_day}}, {{first_name}}! Here's everything you need to know # Option C — Action-focused Action required: Confirm your {{appointment_date}} call with {{agent_name}}

Step 4: Build your reminder sequence

Reminder sequences are the single most effective way to increase show rates. Our data shows 3-touch reminder sequences increase show rates from ~55% to 82%+.

When sent Message
24 hrs before Hey {{first_name}}, just a reminder — we're meeting {{appointment_day}} at {{appointment_time}}. Looking forward to it! Reply RESCHEDULE if you need to change the time.
2 hrs before Almost time! Your call with {{agent_name}} starts in 2 hours at {{appointment_time}}. Here's your link: {{meeting_link}} 🎯
15 min before Starting in 15 minutes, {{first_name}}! Get ready: {{meeting_link}}
If no-show Hey {{first_name}}, it looks like we missed our call. No worries — I have another slot available. Want to reschedule? {{calendar_link}}

💡 No-show recovery

Enable "No-Show Recovery" automation. If a lead misses their appointment, the AI automatically sends 2 recovery messages over the next 24 hours with a direct reschedule link. This recovers 30–40% of no-shows.

Meeting types and locations

Configure what type of meeting is booked and where it takes place. NovaOps supports:

  • →Video call — Automatically generate Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams links per booking.
  • →Phone call — Collect the lead's phone number and include it in your calendar event.
  • →In-person — Include your office or service location address in confirmations and reminders.
  • →On-site visit — Collect the lead's address for in-home or on-site service businesses (roofing, solar, HVAC, etc.).
