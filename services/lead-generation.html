What We Build

A full lead generation engine

Not just leads — qualified, ready-to-buy prospects delivered directly to your calendar. Here's how we do it.

📡

Multi-Channel Outreach

We reach your ideal customers across email, LinkedIn, SMS, and paid channels simultaneously. Each channel is optimized independently, creating a consistent pipeline from multiple sources.

🤖

AI Qualification Engine

Every lead is scored and qualified by AI before entering your CRM. Only prospects who match your ICP — with the right budget, timeline, and intent — make it onto your calendar.

📈

CRM Integration & Weekly Reports

Qualified leads flow automatically into your CRM with full context. Every Monday you receive a pipeline report showing lead volume, quality scores, conversion rates, and optimizations made.

Process

From zero to full pipeline in 14 days

A structured 4-step process that gets your lead generation engine running fast.

STEP 01

Target ICP Definition

We work with you to define your ideal customer profile — industry, company size, job title, pain points, and buying signals. This is the foundation everything else is built on.

STEP 02

Outreach Build

We build your multi-channel outreach sequences — email scripts, LinkedIn connection flows, SMS follow-ups, and ad targeting. All copy is written, tested, and ready to go.

STEP 03

AI Follow-Up

AI takes over the moment a lead responds. It qualifies, nurtures, and handles objections automatically — routing only the right leads forward to your calendar.

STEP 04

Qualified to Calendar

Qualified leads book directly into your calendar with all context attached. You show up to calls already knowing who you're talking to and what they need.
