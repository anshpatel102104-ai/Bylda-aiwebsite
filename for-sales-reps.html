Most reps are coached almost never

The honest version of sales coaching in most organizations: a few sessions a quarter, based on whichever call your manager happened to sit in on, delivered as general advice you have heard before. Everything else is you guessing about your own habits — which is impossible, because you cannot hear yourself the way a buyer does.

The information you need already exists. It is in your own calls. Nobody has the hours to go and find it.

You are not missing effort. You are missing a mirror.

What you get instead

Four things, ranked, short enough to read with coffee:

  • Your strongest behavior — named specifically, so you can repeat it on purpose instead of by accident.
  • Your biggest leak — with the timestamp, so you can hear it yourself and decide whether you agree.
  • The pattern — what is repeating across your conversations, not a one-off from a bad Tuesday.
  • One directive — a single specific change for today. Not five. Not a scorecard.

Where the money is

The leak is almost always a sequence, not a skill.

Reps expect to be told they need to be more persuasive. Far more often, the finding is about order and timing.

Price arriving before decision criteria. An objection answered in a second and a half, at length, instead of with a question. A demo given before anyone confirmed what they were evaluating. None of these are character flaws. They are habits, they are visible in the recording, and they are genuinely fixable in a week.
