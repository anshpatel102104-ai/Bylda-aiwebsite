How It Works

How NovaOps CRM Automation works.

1

Lead enters your pipeline from any source

Website form, Facebook ad, Google Ad, cold email reply, inbound call — every entry point is monitored. The moment a lead arrives, automation kicks in within seconds.

2

AI instantly tags, scores, and categorizes the lead

AI reads the lead data, enriches it with company info, LinkedIn, and intent signals, then assigns a lead score and the correct pipeline stage. No human needed.

3

Automated sequences trigger based on lead status

Hot lead? Immediate outreach sequence. Warm lead? Nurture drip. Qualified but not ready? Long-term follow-up campaign. Every lead gets the right touch at the right time.

4

AI sends follow-ups, books appointments, updates records

Follow-up emails, SMS messages, and appointment requests go out automatically. When a lead responds or books, the CRM record updates instantly. Every action is logged.

5

You get a daily digest of pipeline activity

Each morning, you receive a clean summary: new leads, pipeline movement, follow-up outcomes, and any leads that need your personal attention. Your CRM is always up to date — without you touching it.

FAQ

Common questions answered.

Does CRM Automation replace my existing CRM? +
No — it enhances it. NovaOps AI connects directly to your existing CRM and automates the work inside it. We never replace your tool; we make it work smarter. Your team keeps using the same interface they know.
Which CRMs do you support? +
We support 50+ CRMs including GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Close CRM, ActiveCampaign, Zoho, Keap, Monday.com, Airtable, and many more. If you use a CRM not on our list, reach out — we've integrated with many niche platforms as well.
How long does setup take? +
Most clients go live in 7–14 days. We handle the full build — CRM connection, automation mapping, sequence setup, and testing. You'll get a walkthrough before launch so you understand exactly how everything works.
Can I customize the automation flows? +
Yes — everything is customized to your business. Your pipeline stages, lead scoring criteria, follow-up sequences, and messaging are all built specifically for your sales process. Nothing is generic or cookie-cutter.
What happens to my existing CRM data? +
Your existing data stays exactly where it is. We connect to your CRM via API and only add automation on top — we don't modify, move, or delete any of your current records. Existing contacts can also be enrolled in automation sequences if desired.
