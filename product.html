What Bylda is not

Worth being direct, because the category is crowded and several adjacent products look superficially similar.

  • Not a CRM. Yours stays the system of record. Bylda reads from it and writes behavioral context back. No migration. See integrations.
  • Not a call recorder. Recording is an input, and a commoditized one. The product is the finding, not the archive.
  • Not a dashboard. Dashboards report numbers and leave the interpretation to you. Bylda returns a ranked finding and the change it implies.
  • Not a chatbot. There is no box to ask questions into and hope. The audit arrives whether or not anyone opens the product.
  • Not an emotion detector. Bylda measures observable conduct. It does not score how anyone felt — see what it does and does not read.
