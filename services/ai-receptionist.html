The Problem

Every missed call is
revenue walking out the door.

Service businesses miss 30–40% of inbound calls. A caller who goes to voicemail calls the next business on Google. They don't leave a message and wait. They move on. Beyond missed calls, most businesses don't have the bandwidth to handle high call volume during peak hours — which means rushed conversations, poor qualification, and inconsistent booking rates. An AI receptionist solves all three problems, permanently.

Features

Your always-on receptionist.
No sick days. No hold music.

📞

Inbound Call Handling

Every call answered on the first ring, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Your AI greets callers with your brand name, understands their need, and handles it — without putting them on hold.

📅

Appointment Booking by Phone

Books directly into your real calendar during the call. Checks availability in real-time, offers options, and confirms the booking with a text — while the caller is still on the line.

❓

FAQ Answering

Handles your most common questions — pricing, services, location, hours, process — accurately and consistently. No more staff time spent answering the same five questions 20 times a day.

🔀

Intelligent Call Routing

Hot prospects who need immediate human attention are transferred to the right person instantly, with a full briefing. Emergency calls, VIP clients, and custom routing rules are all configurable.

📋

CRM Logging

Full call transcript, summary, contact details, and lead score automatically logged to your CRM after every call. Your team sees everything — without listening to a single voicemail.

📤

Outbound Follow-Up Calls

Your AI also makes outbound calls — following up with new leads, confirming appointments, re-engaging no-contact prospects. Outbound without the overhead of a calling team.
