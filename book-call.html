About the Call

Everything you need to know before you book.

  • We spend the first 10 minutes understanding your business, goals, and current tech stack. The next 15 minutes we walk through a live audit of your operations — spotting the manual tasks and missed revenue opportunities. The final 5 minutes we outline a prioritized automation roadmap you keep regardless of what you decide next.

  • You'll speak directly with Ansh or a senior member of the NovaOps AI team — not a junior sales rep. Every person who joins strategy calls has hands-on experience building and deploying AI automation systems for real businesses.

  • We schedule 30 minutes and respect your time completely. Most calls wrap up right at 30 minutes. If there's more to explore and you want to continue, we can, but we'll never go over without your explicit invitation.

  • Nothing formal required. It helps to have a rough sense of: (1) your monthly lead volume, (2) your biggest time-draining tasks, and (3) which tools you currently use (CRM, scheduling, etc.). But even if you come with nothing, we'll guide the conversation and uncover everything we need together.
