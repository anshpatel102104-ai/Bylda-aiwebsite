Changelog

What shipped.

Every release that changed what Bylda does for a rep.

July 2026Microsoft Dynamics, two-way

Full read and write against Dynamics 365 Sales, including custom entities and the same field-mapping approval flow as Salesforce.

June 2026Buying committee mapping

Bylda now tracks every stakeholder named across the whole account history, flags who is missing from the deal, and warns when a champion goes quiet.

June 2026Forecast grounded in conversations

Deal confidence is now derived from what was said rather than stage age. Every number in the roll-up traces to a timestamp in a real call.

May 2026EU and UK data residency

Choose where your data is processed and stored, including transcripts, embeddings, and backups.

May 2026Follow-ups in your voice

Drafts now learn each rep's tone from their own sent mail instead of applying one house style to the whole team.

April 2026SOC 2 Type II

Completed our Type II audit across security, availability, and confidentiality. Report available under NDA.

March 2026Morning Brief

The daily ranked brief replaced our notification digest. Prepared overnight, ordered by revenue impact, with the work already drafted underneath each item.
