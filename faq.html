FAQ

Everything, answered plainly.

Including the questions with awkward answers.

Bylda is behavioral sales intelligence software. It turns recorded sales calls and CRM activity into behaviors, patterns and outcomes, gives each rep one change to make, and measures whether the behavior changed. It sits alongside the CRM, dialer and call recorder you already use rather than replacing them.

A CRM records activity and stores what someone typed after the fact. Bylda measures the conduct inside the conversations that produced those records — how much discovery happened, how objections were handled, where pricing landed relative to decision criteria, who actually attended. Your CRM stays the system of record; Bylda is the system of intelligence on top of it.

Conversation intelligence analyzes one call at a time and hands a human per-call metrics to interpret. Bylda treats behavior repeating across many calls, reps and outcomes as the unit of analysis, returns one ranked directive with the evidence behind it, and then uses what happened next to judge whether that recommendation was right. Bylda is not a call library, so many teams run both.

Bylda reads calls and activity from your CRM, dialer or call recorder, or from a manual upload. Salesforce and HubSpot are the two we document in detail. Writing behavioral context back into the CRM is on the roadmap, not in the first release. If you run something else, ask us.
