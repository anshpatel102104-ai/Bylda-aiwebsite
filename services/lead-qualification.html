How It Works

5 steps from lead to
qualified booking.

1

Lead enters your pipeline

Any source — form fill, Facebook ad, Google Ad, referral, inbound call, organic inquiry. Every lead triggers the qualification system automatically the moment they come in.

2

AI sends qualification sequence within 60 seconds

SMS and/or email — a personalized, natural-sounding opening message that starts a conversation without sounding like a bot or a form. The lead thinks they're talking to a real person.

3

AI asks BANT questions through conversation

Budget: do they have the resources? Authority: are they the decision maker? Need: does your solution actually solve their problem? Timeline: are they buying now or "someday"? All gathered through natural back-and-forth — not a survey.

4

AI scores the lead 1–10 against your ICP

Every answer is weighted against your ideal customer profile. A lead score is calculated in real time. CRM is updated with score, summary, and all qualification data — automatically.

5

Score determines the next step — automatically

No manual review needed. The system routes each lead to the right outcome based on their score, right away.

Score 7–10 — Hot Lead
Books directly to your calendar

AI offers available times from your real calendar. Lead picks, confirms, and you get a notification immediately.

Score 4–6 — Warm Lead
Enters nurture with re-qualification touchpoints

Automated follow-up sequence designed to keep them warm and re-qualify when their situation changes.

Score 1–3 — Poor Fit
Archived with reason logged in CRM

Disqualification reason is recorded in your CRM. Long-term re-engagement can be triggered later if criteria change.

FAQ

Questions about
AI Lead Qualification.

How is this different from a lead scoring tool I set up in my CRM?
CRM lead scoring typically uses passive signals — page visits, email opens, form fields. Our AI qualification is active: it actually engages the lead in a two-way conversation, asks direct questions, and gathers real answers about budget, decision authority, timeline, and need. The lead tells the AI what they need to tell you. No guessing from behavior signals.
Can I control exactly what questions the AI asks?
Yes — fully. We build your qualification script in your onboarding call, based on your offer, your pricing, and your ideal customer. You review every question before it goes live. You can also update the script at any time if your criteria change or you find a better question that converts.
What happens to leads that don't qualify?
Score 1–3: archived with a logged disqualification reason in your CRM. They're not deleted — they exist in a long-term list that can be re-activated later. Score 4–6: enter an automated nurture sequence with re-qualification touchpoints at 30, 60, and 90 days. These medium-score leads often convert once their situation changes.
What industries does this work best for?
Any high-ticket service where one qualified meeting is worth $1,000+. The system has the highest ROI for Real Estate, Solar, Insurance, Med Spa, Coaching, and Agencies — where the cost of an unqualified call in staff time and morale is significant. We have pre-built scripts for these industries that can be deployed faster.
Will leads know they're talking to AI?
The AI introduces itself as an automated assistant for your business — it's transparent about being AI-powered, but the conversation is natural, helpful, and respectful. Response rates are consistently high because leads understand the AI is there to help them get to the right person quickly, not to trick them.
