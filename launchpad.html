FAQ

Common questions
answered.

Are the tools really free?
Yes — all 20 Launchpad tools are free to use with no credit card required. You get full access to every tool in the hub. When you're ready to add done-for-you AI automation systems (appointment setting, CRM automation, Voice AI, etc.), those are available on paid plans. See pricing →
What AI powers these tools?
Launchpad tools are built on large language models from leading AI providers, fine-tuned specifically for founders and business builders. The models are optimized to generate actionable, business-specific output — not generic text. You get results tailored to your idea, industry, and stage.
How is this different from ChatGPT?
ChatGPT is a general-purpose assistant. Launchpad tools are purpose-built workflows for specific founder tasks — validating an idea, generating a GTM strategy, finding your first customers. Each tool has a structured input form, the right prompting behind the scenes, and output formatted for immediate use. No prompt engineering required.
Can I export my results?
Yes. All tool outputs can be copied to your clipboard instantly. PDF and DOCX export is available on the Launch plan ($49/mo) and above. Your results are also saved in your dashboard so you can revisit and iterate anytime.
How do I upgrade to get automation?
When you're ready to go beyond the free tools and add AI automation to your business, visit our pricing page to choose an automation plan. All plans include done-for-you setup — our team configures everything in your existing CRM and calendar tools. No technical knowledge required.
