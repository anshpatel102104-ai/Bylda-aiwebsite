General

NovaOps AI is the AI Operating System for founders. We combine 17 AI tools (for idea validation, pitching, finding customers) with 6 done-for-you automation systems that run your lead generation, follow-up, CRM, and operations on autopilot. Think of us as your entire ops team, powered by AI.

We work with ambitious founders, agency owners, and service businesses who want to scale without hiring. Our clients include real estate agents, insurance agencies, med spas, solar companies, home services businesses, roofers, and SaaS founders.
