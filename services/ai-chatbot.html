Features

A chatbot that's actually
built to convert.

💬

Instant Website Engagement

The chatbot greets visitors with a relevant opening message within seconds of landing on your site. Triggered by page, time on site, or scroll depth — reaching the right visitors at the right moment.

📋

Lead Capture Forms

Collects name, email, phone, and any custom fields conversationally — not with a cold form. Leads are captured even if the visitor doesn't complete the booking, giving you a follow-up list.

✅

Qualification Questions

Your chatbot asks your qualification questions naturally in conversation. By the time the lead reaches you, you already know their budget, timeline, service need, and location.

Live Chat Handoff

Hot prospects can be transferred to a live team member with one click — with full conversation history. Your team jumps in at exactly the right moment, fully informed.

Calendar Booking In Chat

Visitors book appointments directly inside the chat window. No redirect, no form, no friction. Connected to your real calendar — only shows times that are actually available.

CRM Sync

Every lead, every conversation, and every booking syncs to your CRM automatically. Leads are tagged, scored, and entered into the appropriate follow-up sequence without manual data entry.
