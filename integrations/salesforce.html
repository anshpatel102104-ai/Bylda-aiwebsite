What Bylda does with Salesforce

Bylda connects over OAuth, reads your objects and schema — standard and custom — and maps meaning to your fields. You review and approve the mapping before the first write. From then on, the record maintains itself:

  • Opportunities stay current. Stage changes with the quote that justified them, close dates moved because someone said a date out loud, amounts and next steps that match what was agreed.
  • Contact roles build themselves. Stakeholders are added and roled the moment they're named on a call — economic buyer, champion, procurement.
  • Activity logs write themselves. Calls, emails, and meetings land as structured activity with a summary, not a transcript dump in a notes field.
  • Every write is accountable. Logged, attributed to a timestamp in the source conversation, and reversible from an audit trail.
Salesforce stops being a chore your team maintains and becomes a record that maintains itself.

Then the intelligence layer goes to work

Because Bylda sees both the record and the behavior underneath it, it can do what a report builder can't: explain the pipeline. Which deals are quietly stalling and why. Where the decision maker never showed. Which objection is spreading across accounts. Each flag arrives with its evidence and a recommended next move — that's deal intelligence, running on your own Salesforce data.
