The Problem

Empty chair. No revenue.
Fully preventable.

Most no-shows aren't intentional. People forget. They get busy. They mean to reschedule but don't. And when they don't show, you're left with blocked time, no revenue, and no chance to fill the slot.

The solution isn't calling every client manually — that's unrealistic at scale. It's an automated reminder chain that reminds at the right times, makes it dead simple to confirm or reschedule, and fills gaps before they happen.

The 3-Touch System

Timed exactly right.
Three reminders. Zero no-shows.

24h

First Touch — 24 Hours Before

A friendly confirmation message sent 24 hours before the appointment. Clear confirmation link and a one-click reschedule option. Most no-shows are prevented here — people who need to cancel have 24 hours to do it, freeing up the slot for someone else.

2h

Second Touch — 2 Hours Before

A shorter, higher-urgency SMS reminder 2 hours out. Includes location, parking instructions, and what to bring. Catches the people who forgot to respond to the 24-hour reminder. Simple, direct, effective.

15m

Third Touch — 15 Minutes Before

A final "on my way?" check-in 15 minutes before the appointment. Humanizes the process and gives last-minute no-shows a graceful exit — and gives you enough time to attempt to fill the slot if needed.

One-Click Reschedule

Every reminder includes a reschedule link. One tap opens your live calendar — they pick a new time, it books automatically, and you get notified. Cancellations become reschedules. Lost revenue becomes deferred revenue.
