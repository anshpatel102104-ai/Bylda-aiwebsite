Getting Started

What is NovaOps AI? +

NovaOps AI is the AI Operating System for founders. We combine 20 AI-powered tools for idea validation, business planning, and customer acquisition with 6 done-for-you automation systems that run your lead generation, follow-up, CRM, and operations on autopilot — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Think of us as your entire ops team and strategy department, powered by AI. Most founders using NovaOps save 15–22 hours per week on manual work and see measurable revenue results within the first 30 days.

Who is NovaOps AI built for? +

We built NovaOps for two types of people:

  • Early-stage founders who want to validate their idea, build a business plan, and find their first customers using AI — fast.
  • Established service businesses (5–50 employees) who want to automate lead follow-up, appointment setting, CRM workflows, and operations without hiring more staff.

Our clients include real estate agents, insurance agencies, med spas, solar companies, HVAC companies, roofers, plumbers, landscapers, chiropractors, dentists, and SaaS founders.

How do I get started? +

There are two ways to get started with NovaOps:

  • Free — Try the tools: Create a free account and access 6 AI tools in Launchpad instantly. No credit card needed.
  • Paid — Get the full system: Book a free strategy call. Our team will audit your business, design your automation stack, and have you live in 14 days.

Most clients start with the free tools, see immediate value, then upgrade to the full automation suite.

Do I need technical skills or coding knowledge? +

None whatsoever. NovaOps is built for operators and founders, not developers. You do not need to write code, configure APIs, or understand any technical concepts.

For the automation systems, our team handles 100% of the technical setup — API connections, webhook configuration, integrations, testing, and QA. Your only job is to review and approve the flows before we go live.

For the Launchpad AI tools, you simply fill in the form fields and the AI does the rest.

How long does it take to get set up? +

Most clients are fully live within 14 days of their first strategy call. Here is the typical timeline:

  • Days 1–2: Discovery audit — we map your current workflows and define automation priorities
  • Days 3–7: Build phase — our team builds all automation sequences, scripts, and integrations
  • Days 8–12: Integration and testing — we connect everything to your CRM, calendar, and phone system
  • Days 13–14: Launch and handoff — we go live and brief your team on what to expect

Some simple systems can go live in as few as 5 days. Complex multi-system builds may take up to 21 days.

Is there a free plan? +

Yes. Our Launchpad Free plan gives you permanent access to 6 of our AI tools at no cost — no credit card required, no time limit.

The free plan includes our most popular tools for early-stage founders: the Idea Validator, the Business Name Generator, the Elevator Pitch Builder, the Niche Finder, the Target Customer Profiler, and the First 10 Customers brainstorm tool.

To unlock all 20 AI tools plus the automation systems, upgrade to any paid plan at any time.

What tools are included in the free plan? +

The free Launchpad plan includes these 6 AI tools:

Idea Validator Business Name Generator Elevator Pitch Builder Niche Finder Target Customer Profiler First 10 Customers Finder

All 20 AI tools are unlocked on paid plans, starting from our Launch plan.

Section 2

The Platform & Tools

What are the 20 AI tools in Launchpad? +

Launchpad is our browser-based AI toolkit for founders. The 20 tools are organized into four phases of business building:

  • Validate: Idea Validator, Kill My Idea, Niche Finder, Competitor Analyzer, Market Size Estimator
  • Plan: Business Plan Generator, GTM Strategy Builder, Pricing Strategy Builder, Revenue Model Builder, Financial Projections Tool
  • Launch: Elevator Pitch Builder, Investor Pitch Deck Outline, Business Name Generator, Target Customer Profiler
  • Grow: First 10 Customers Finder, Cold Email Generator, LinkedIn Outreach Builder

All tools are available at app.launchpad.nova-ops.space.

What is the Idea Validator and how does it work? +

The Idea Validator takes your business idea — described in plain English — and runs it through a structured analysis framework in seconds. You get back:

  • A viability score (1–10) with detailed reasoning
  • Market size estimate and target customer profile
  • Top 3 risks and how to mitigate them
  • Whether to build, pivot, or kill the idea
  • Suggested next steps if you move forward

It takes about 30 seconds to fill in the form and 15 seconds for the AI to generate a full analysis. Most founders run 3–5 ideas through it in a single session.

How does the Business Plan Generator work? +

The Business Plan Generator produces a full, investor-ready business plan from a short form you fill in. You provide your business idea, target market, revenue model, and a few other inputs — the AI produces a structured plan covering:

  • Executive summary
  • Problem and solution statement
  • Market analysis and competitive landscape
  • Revenue model and pricing strategy
  • Go-to-market strategy
  • Financial projections (Year 1–3)
  • Team and operational plan

The generated plan can be exported as a PDF or copied directly into your own document.

What is the GTM Strategy Builder? +

The Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy Builder creates a 90-day launch roadmap tailored to your specific business, industry, and target customer. Unlike generic marketing advice, it factors in your budget, team size, and competitive environment.

The output includes your top 3 acquisition channels, weekly action steps for the first 90 days, content strategy outline, and suggested KPIs to track progress. Most founders use this alongside the Business Plan Generator to prepare for investor conversations.

How does the First 10 Customers Finder work? +

The hardest part of any new business is getting the first paying customers. This tool generates a step-by-step acquisition plan specifically for your offer and customer type.

You describe your product or service and your ideal customer, and the AI outputs: where to find them (platforms, communities, events), what to say to get their attention, and a prioritized sequence of outreach actions to land your first 10 paying customers — without paid ads.

What is the Kill My Idea tool? +

Kill My Idea is our most brutally honest tool. It plays devil's advocate — taking your business idea and generating every reason it could fail. Think of it as stress-testing your idea before you invest time and money into it.

It surfaces competitive threats you may not have considered, revenue model weaknesses, customer acquisition challenges, and market timing risks. Many founders use it immediately after the Idea Validator to get the full picture: both the upside case and the realistic downside scenario.

Can I export my business plan as a PDF? +

Yes. All outputs from the Launchpad tools — including the Business Plan Generator — can be exported as formatted PDFs or copied to your clipboard. PDF export is available on the Launch plan and above. Free plan users can copy the full text output.

How accurate are the AI outputs? +

Our AI tools are built on state-of-the-art language models trained on large volumes of business, marketing, and entrepreneurship content. The outputs are high-quality starting points — most founders treat them as a strong first draft and refine from there.

That said, AI doesn't have access to real-time market data or your specific financial history. We recommend treating outputs as expert-level strategic frameworks and validating market size numbers with your own primary research. The tools are substantially better than starting from scratch and better than most generic business plan templates.

Automation Systems

What are the 6 automation systems? +

Our 6 done-for-you automation systems cover the entire lead-to-customer lifecycle:

AI Appointment Setting Missed Call Text-Back AI Voice Calling CRM Pipeline Automation AI Lead Nurturing Review & Reputation System

Each system is fully built, integrated, tested, and managed for you. Higher-tier plans include more systems running simultaneously from day one.

What is AI Appointment Setting? +

AI Appointment Setting is an automated system that contacts your leads via SMS and email, qualifies them through a natural conversation, and books them directly into your calendar — without a human rep making a single outbound call.

When a new lead comes in, the AI sends the first message within 90 seconds (the critical window where response rates are highest), then follows up persistently over days and weeks using proven sequences. It handles objections, answers common FAQs, and confirms appointments automatically. Most clients see a 40–60% increase in booked appointments within the first 30 days.

How does Missed Call Text-Back work? +

Every missed call to your business is a potential lost customer. Missed Call Text-Back automatically sends a personalized SMS within seconds of a missed call — before the caller dials a competitor.

The message is conversational, introduces your business, and invites them to reply or book a time to talk. The AI then handles the conversation, qualifies the lead, and routes them to your calendar or team. Clients typically recover 15–40% of missed calls as booked appointments that would otherwise have been lost permanently.

What is AI voice calling? +

AI voice calling uses advanced conversational AI to make and receive phone calls on behalf of your business. Common use cases include:

  • Appointment confirmation and reminder calls
  • Lead qualification calls (outbound)
  • Re-engagement calls for cold or dormant leads
  • After-hours inbound call handling

The AI voice sounds natural, handles dynamic conversations in real time, and hands off to a human rep when the lead is qualified and ready to move forward. Availability depends on your plan.

How does CRM automation work? +

CRM automation connects your lead sources, communication channels, and sales pipeline so that every lead moves through stages automatically — without manual data entry or task management on your team's part.

We build custom automation rules inside your CRM that: assign leads to reps, move deals to the next stage based on activity, trigger follow-up sequences, create tasks for your team, and send you daily pipeline reports. The result is a CRM that manages itself, giving you full visibility into every deal with minimal manual overhead.

What integrations does NovaOps support? +

We support 50+ integrations including:

  • CRMs: GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Close CRM, Zoho, ActiveCampaign
  • Calendars: Calendly, Cal.com, Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar
  • Communication: Twilio, RingCentral, Dialpad, Gmail, Outlook
  • Automation platforms: Zapier, Make (Integromat), n8n
  • Payments: Stripe, Square, QuickBooks
  • Ad platforms: Meta Ads, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads (lead form integration)

If you use a tool not listed here, ask us — there is a good chance we can support it.

Can I use NovaOps with GoHighLevel / HubSpot / Salesforce? +

Yes to all three. GoHighLevel is actually our most common platform — we build natively inside GHL and can supercharge any existing GHL account with our automation systems. HubSpot and Salesforce are also fully supported with native API integrations.

If you are not on any platform yet and starting fresh, we will recommend the best CRM for your specific use case during the discovery call and help you get it fully configured as part of your onboarding at no extra cost.

How long before I see results from automation? +

Most clients see measurable results in their first 2–4 weeks of automation being live. Common early wins:

  • Recovered leads from missed calls (often within the first 24 hours of launch)
  • Increased appointment booking rate from existing lead volume
  • Reduction in no-show rate from automated reminders and confirmations
  • Hours saved per week on manual follow-up tasks

Compounding results — a full pipeline of warm leads and consistent month-over-month growth — typically kick in by weeks 6–12 as the system optimizes and more leads flow through the pipeline.

We offer four tiers. Full details are on our pricing page, but here is the summary:

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts required. You can cancel at any time from your account dashboard, and you will retain full access until the end of your current billing period. There are no cancellation fees or penalties whatsoever.

For annual subscribers, cancellation stops future renewal. We do not prorate refunds for unused months on annual plans, unless the cancellation falls within the 30-day money-back guarantee window.

Is there a setup fee? +

No setup fees, ever, on any plan. The price you see on the pricing page is the price you pay. We handle all integration, configuration, and system builds as part of your subscription — no hidden onboarding charge added at checkout.

What is the 30-day money-back guarantee? +

If you are not satisfied with NovaOps AI within the first 30 days of any paid plan, we will refund your full payment — no questions asked. Because there are no setup fees, there is nothing held back: you get every dollar back. To claim your refund, simply email us at ansh@nova-ops.space within 30 days of your payment date.

What is the 14-day live guarantee? +

On the Scale plan, we guarantee your automation systems will be fully built, integrated, tested, and live within 14 days of your first strategy call. If we miss that deadline due to anything on our end, we work for free until everything is running — no credits, no partial fixes, we finish the job at no extra cost to you.

This guarantee exists because we know speed matters. Every day without automation is revenue left on the table.

Do you offer annual billing discounts? +

Yes. Toggle to annual billing to save 20% on every paid plan — Launch drops from $49 to $39/month, Operate from $149 to $119/month, and Scale from $299 to $239/month.
