Case Study

Landscaping Company Doubled Recurring Revenue in One Season

GreenScape Pro was stuck on one-time jobs with no system to convert customers into recurring contracts. After deploying quote follow-up and seasonal reactivation, they doubled their recurring client base in a single season.

The Problem

Stuck in the One-Time Job Trap

🔁

One-Time Jobs Only

Most clients hired GreenScape for a single cleanup and never returned. With no system to propose or follow up on recurring contracts, the business had to find new customers every month.

📃

No Quote Follow-Up

Quotes went out and sat in inboxes. Amanda had no time to chase every unanswered proposal, so most expired without a response — even when the customer was still interested.

❄️

Lost Clients After Winter

Each spring, Amanda had to rebuild her client roster from scratch. Past customers who'd moved on or forgotten weren't being re-engaged — leaving a goldmine of warm relationships untapped.

What We Built

Turn One-Time Jobs Into Lifetime Clients

Step 01

Quote Follow-Up Sequences

Every quote triggered a 5-touch follow-up: a reminder at day 2, a value-add message at day 5, an urgency prompt at day 10, and a final check-in at day 14 — fully automated.

Step 02

Seasonal Reactivation Campaign

At the start of each season, a personalized campaign went out to every past client. Messages referenced their previous work and offered a spring/fall maintenance package with an easy rebooking link.

Step 03

Review Collection System

After each service visit, clients received a thank-you text and review request. Five-star responses were directed to Google, keeping the profile fresh and boosting local search rankings.

The Outcome

20 Recurring Clients to 45. In One Season.
