The Challenges

What's quietly costing your shop thousands every month

🚗

Empty Bays and No-Shows

A no-show doesn't just mean an empty bay for an hour — it means a technician gets paid for downtime, the next potential booking was turned away, and you've lost the revenue twice over. Without automated reminders, no-show rates average 15–20%.

📱

Slow Appointment Booking

When a customer calls and has to leave a voicemail or sit on hold, a meaningful percentage hangs up and searches for the next shop. Online booking helps, but only if someone is responding to inquiries within minutes — not hours.

😴

Customer Win-Back Gaps

Most auto repair shops have thousands of customers who came in once or twice and never returned. They didn't have a bad experience — they just forgot. A systematic win-back program targeting oil change intervals and mileage milestones turns lapsed customers into regulars.

How NovaOps Helps

Fill every bay with AI-powered automation

🔔

Reminder Automation

Multi-step appointment reminders sent 48 hrs, 24 hrs, and 2 hrs before each appointment — with easy one-tap confirm or reschedule. No-show rates drop by 50% on average, and your shop can backfill any opening from a waitlist automatically.

📲

Missed Call Textback

Every missed call from a potential customer triggers an immediate text response with a booking link. Your front desk can't always pick up, but the AI never misses a call. Appointment volume doubles for most shops within 90 days of going live.

🔄

Lapsed Customer Reactivation

Customers who haven't visited in 6 months automatically receive a service reminder tied to their last visit mileage and service type. Oil change due reminders, tire rotation prompts, and seasonal check-up offers bring them back before they search Google. Return rates improve 1.8x.
