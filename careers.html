Culture

How We Work

⚡

Ship Fast, Learn Faster

We have a strong bias for action. Ideas ship in days, not sprints. We embrace imperfection, iterate quickly, and let real-world results guide our decisions.

🏗️

Founders First

Everything we build is in service of our users — ambitious founders trying to build something. If it doesn't move the needle for them, we don't build it.

🌐

Remote & Async by Default

We're fully remote, async-first, and trust you to manage your own time. Deep work is sacred. Meetings exist only when truly necessary.
