Insurance Agency Stops Leaving $14k/Month on the Table

A 3-agent independent insurance agency was following up with barely 40% of their inbound quote requests. NovaOps AI automated the entire lead pipeline, recovered $14k in previously lost revenue in the first month, and gave every agent back 15 hours per week.

Independent Insurance Agency 3 Agents Personal + Commercial Lines 45% More Quotes Converted
The Problem

Agents Chasing Active Clients While New Leads Went Cold

In a 3-agent shop, bandwidth is everything. The agents were so tied up managing existing accounts that new leads — especially internet-sourced quote requests — were consistently falling through the cracks.

📧
Quote requests not followed up within 24 hours. Internet leads from comparison sites and the agency website expect a callback within the hour. This agency was averaging 28-hour follow-up times — by which point, 60% of prospects had already chosen a competitor or stopped shopping entirely.
❄️
60% of internet leads going cold before contact. Quote shoppers move fast. Without a system to respond instantly and nurture the prospect through the decision process, the majority of leads were never actually quoted. The agency was paying for lead generation that delivered little return.
🗂️
Agents spending 15+ hours per week on admin tasks. Between renewal reminders, policy change follow-ups, and lead callbacks, agents were burning nearly half their selling time on low-leverage administrative work. That time could have been spent closing new business.
The Solution

End-to-End Insurance Lead Automation

NovaOps AI built a full-funnel automation system specifically designed for insurance agencies — from the instant a lead arrives through policy renewal. Every stage was automated to keep agents focused exclusively on high-value conversations.

✓
Instant Quote Follow-Up — Every quote request triggers an immediate AI response within 60 seconds, acknowledging the request, setting expectations for timeline, and collecting any additional information needed. Agents are notified in real-time when a qualified prospect is ready.
✓
AI Lead Qualification — Before an agent spends time on a quote, the AI asks qualifying questions via SMS: coverage type, current carrier, reason for shopping, budget range. Unqualified tire-kickers are filtered automatically, letting agents focus on real buyers.
✓
Renewal Reminder Sequences — 90, 60, and 30 days before policy renewal, automated messages go out reminding clients to review their coverage — and prompting a check-in call. Renewal retention improved by 18% in the first quarter.
✓
CRM Automation & Admin Elimination — Policy updates, document requests, and standard follow-ups were automated via CRM workflows. Agents saved an average of 15 hours per week that was redirected entirely to new business development.
