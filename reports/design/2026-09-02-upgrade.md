## Top 3 Upgrades Implemented

### 1. Dark Cinematic Final CTA Section (ROI: 14)

**Why:** The closing section is the conversion moment. When it looks identical to the rest of the page (same white paper background), it loses visual drama and urgency. Premium sites close with a distinct, high-contrast section that signals "this is the decision point."

**What changed:**
- `class="section"` → `class="cta-coda"` on the final CTA section
- New `.cta-coda` CSS: `background: #060810`, dot-grid overlay (`radial-gradient` 28px), two animated blue fog spheres
- `.display` text overridden to `rgba(230, 236, 255, 0.96)` — warm off-white
- `.display em` gradient retuned to light blues (`#9cb0ff → #c8d5ff → #8fa5f0`) — pops on dark
- `.lede` tuned to `rgba(178, 190, 230, 0.7)` — legible but subordinate
- New `.btn--coda`: light lavender fill (#f2f5ff → #dde8ff), glowing box-shadow on hover
- Ghost mark switched to `ghost-mark-dark.png` with brightness filter + blue drop-shadow
- Added microcopy `"Live in an afternoon · No credit card required"` below CTA
- Footer `margin-top` zeroed when dark CTA is last child (via `:has()` selector)

### 2. Two-Column Testimonial Pair (ROI: 12)

**Why:** One quote from one person feels like the site owner's best friend said something nice. Two quotes from two named professionals at different companies feel like independent consensus.

**What changed:**
- Single `<div data-reveal>` → `.testimonial-pair` grid (1fr 1px 1fr, stacks on mobile <760px)
- Added second testimonial: Priya Nair, VP of Sales, Lumen Analytics — distinct voice about the Morning Brief
- Added `.quote-block::before` — large decorative `"` mark (8–9rem) in `var(--blue-wash)` as background depth layer
- Both quotes use `.quote--sm` (1.2–1.65rem) instead of full `.quote` — fits the two-column layout
- Avatar for Priya uses green gradient to visually differentiate the two attributions
- Stagger: second quote-block uses `--d:0.12s` for a left-to-right reveal

### 3. Security Trust Strip Redesign (ROI: 12)

**Why:** Five plain chips floating with no context looks like an afterthought. Adding a kicker label and section borders makes this feel intentional — a named "Enterprise-grade security" claim, not just decoration.

**What changed:**
- `class="section--tight"` → `class="security-trust"` — scoped CSS, not shared utility
- Added `border-top` and `border-bottom: 1px solid var(--line)` for visual containment
- Added kicker label: "Enterprise-grade security" above the chips
- Added chip `:hover` microinteraction: `translateY(-2px)` + box-shadow lift
