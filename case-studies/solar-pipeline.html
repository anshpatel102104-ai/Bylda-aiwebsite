Case Study

Solar Company Built a $4.2M Pipeline in 90 Days

SunBridge Solar had qualified leads but couldn't follow up fast enough. With AI-powered nurture sequences and proposal automation, they scaled their pipeline by millions — without adding headcount.

Client: SunBridge Solar · Rachel T. · Residential Solar Sales

The Problem

Great Leads. Broken Follow-Up.

⏳

Long Sales Cycle

Solar deals take weeks to close. Without a system to stay top-of-mind during that window, most prospects lost interest or signed with a competitor who followed up consistently.

👻

Proposal Ghosting

Proposals were sent and never followed up on. Reps were too busy generating new leads to chase down submitted quotes, leaving revenue on the table every week.

📋

Manual Follow-Up at Scale

The team had grown, but their follow-up process hadn't. Inconsistent outreach meant each rep had a wildly different close rate — and the bottleneck was pure volume.

What We Built

Three Automations. One Scalable Pipeline.

Step 01

Multi-Touch Nurture Sequence

A 12-touch SMS and email sequence kept every lead warm from first inquiry to consultation — with educational content, social proof, and soft CTAs at each stage.

Step 02

AI Proposal Follow-Up

The moment a proposal was sent, a timed follow-up chain launched automatically — nudging prospects at day 2, day 5, and day 10 with objection-handling content.

Step 03

CRM Pipeline Automation

Every interaction — email open, link click, reply — automatically moved deals through the CRM. Sales managers had a real-time view of the entire pipeline with zero manual updates.
