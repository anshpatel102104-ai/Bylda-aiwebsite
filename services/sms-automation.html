FAQ

Questions about SMS Automation.

Is this TCPA compliant?
Yes — full TCPA compliance is built into the platform, not bolted on. We handle express written consent collection at opt-in, automatic opt-out processing (STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT, CANCEL all trigger instant removal), quiet hours enforcement by timezone, and consent timestamping for audit trails. You're covered for both marketing and informational messages. Our legal team reviewed the setup — you don't need to hire a compliance consultant.
Can I customize the messages?
Completely. Every message in every sequence is written specifically for your business — your voice, your offer, your industry language. We start with proven frameworks and then tailor them to your brand during onboarding. You'll review and approve all messages before any sequence goes live. After launch, you can edit any message in the platform dashboard with full version history.
What if someone opts out?
Any inbound STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT, or CANCEL message immediately removes that contact from all active sequences and adds them to a suppression list. They will never receive another automated message from your account. This happens in real-time, every time, automatically — no manual step required. The opt-out is also logged in your CRM contact record for your records.
How does it integrate with my CRM?
We integrate natively with GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, ActiveCampaign, and 50+ other platforms. Every SMS sent and received is logged in your CRM contact timeline automatically. Lead status changes, appointment bookings, and opt-outs all sync in real-time. If you're on a platform not listed, we also support Zapier and Make (Integromat) for custom connections. Setup is handled as part of your onboarding — no developer required.
Can I do MMS (images, videos)?
Yes — full MMS support is included at no extra setup cost. You can send JPEG, PNG, GIF, and MP4 files up to 5MB per message. Common use cases include before/after photos for med spas and contractors, property photos for real estate, video testimonials, and branded promotional graphics for seasonal campaigns. MMS messages have slightly higher carrier fees than SMS, which are passed through at cost. Our team can advise on when MMS outperforms plain SMS based on your use case.
