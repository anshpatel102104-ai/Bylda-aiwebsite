Answers before you start

Which online business model is easiest to start? +

Freelancing and coaching are typically the easiest entry points — you monetize skills you already have, with no inventory or content to create first. Bylda helps you identify your best offer and reach your first clients within days. See how the process works →

Do I need money to start an online business with Bylda? +

No upfront investment required. Bylda is free to start — you get access to AI tools for idea validation, business planning, and finding your first customers. Paid plans unlock ongoing automation and scale features when you're ready.

How long does it take to get my first customer? +

Most founders complete idea validation and business planning in under an hour. Reaching your first paying customer typically takes 7–14 days, depending on your business model. Bylda's 20 free AI tools accelerate every stage from offer creation to customer outreach.

What if I don't know what kind of business to start? +

That's exactly what Bylda is built for. Start free and the AI will ask about your skills, interests, and time available — then recommend the model that fits you best and explain why. You don't need to have it figured out before you begin.

What's the difference between a digital product and a course? +

A digital product is any downloadable file — templates, ebooks, software, or presets. An online course is structured learning content, typically video-based lessons. Both generate passive income.
