The Challenges

The recurring revenue gaps your competitors are filling

📅

Recurring Appointment Chaos

Quarterly and bi-monthly treatment schedules are your backbone revenue — but manually tracking who needs service, calling to schedule, and confirming appointments consumes hours every week and still results in customers slipping through the cracks.

🌡️

Seasonal Lead Overflow

Spring ant season and fall rodent season create lead surges that overwhelm small offices. Leads that aren't contacted within an hour during peak season often go to the first competitor who calls back — and those first treatments are the gateway to recurring contracts.

🌐

No Review Collection System

Pest control is hyperlocal and review-driven — customers search "pest control near me" and choose based on star rating and review count. Without a systematic way to collect reviews after every service, your Google profile stagnates while competitors pull ahead.

How NovaOps Helps

Automate the systems that drive recurring revenue

🔄

Recurring Appointment Automation

The system tracks every customer's service schedule and automatically sends reminders and booking confirmations at the right time. Customers can confirm, reschedule, or add services with one tap. Retention rates climb to 80%+ without additional staff time.

⚡

AI Lead Follow-Up

Every new inquiry — from Google, Angi, or your website — gets an instant response and enters a nurture sequence that moves them from curious to booked. New lead conversion rates double because speed and consistency beat manual outreach every time.

⭐

Post-Treatment Review Requests

After every completed treatment, an automated message asks about the experience. Happy customers are directed to Google in two taps. Our clients build from sub-4-star to 4.8★ average ratings within 90 days — dominating local search results.
