Architecture

One connection. Both directions.

Bylda reads your objects and schema on connect, maps meaning to your fields — custom ones included — and writes back in real time. No middleware, no nightly batch, no CSV.

  • OAuth connection, scoped to what Bylda needs
  • Field mapping you review and approve before first write
  • Writes appear in your CRM within seconds of a call ending
  • Full audit log — every write attributed and reversible
