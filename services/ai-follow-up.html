What's Included

6 follow-up systems running
for you simultaneously.

Each system handles a different stage of your pipeline. Together, they ensure no lead is ever left without a follow-up.

1

New Lead Sequence

7 touchpoints over 14 days — from the moment a lead enters to long-term nurture. Never leaves a new lead without a follow-up.

Day 0 "Hey [name], saw you were interested in [offer]. Is now a good time to connect?"
Day 1 Value-add message with a relevant insight or resource tied to what they asked about.
Day 3 Proactively addresses the most common objection for your service.
Day 5 Social proof — a result story from a similar client in their situation.
Day 7 Direct ask + alternative CTA (call, form, or lower-commitment next step).
Day 10 "Last chance" positioning — creates urgency without being pushy.
Day 14 Moves to long-term nurture sequence if still no response.
2

No-Response Rescue

Pattern-interrupt messages specifically designed for leads who have gone completely silent. Different angle, different channel, different hook — to break through the noise.

Uses behavioral triggers, channel switching, and re-framing to re-engage leads who stopped responding to your initial sequence. Converts leads most businesses have already written off.

3

Appointment Reminder Sequence

Automatically fires before every booked appointment. Three reminders at the right time intervals to maximize show rate.

1 week Confirmation + what to expect at the meeting. Builds anticipation.
24 hrs Reminder with meeting link, any prep materials, and a confirm/reschedule CTA.
2 hrs Final reminder. Link to meeting. Creates urgency. Reduces no-shows dramatically.
4

Post-Appointment Nurture

What happens after the consultation or discovery call matters as much as the call itself. Most businesses go silent. Yours doesn't.

Sends a thank-you, recaps the value discussed, addresses common post-call objections, and keeps the relationship warm for leads who didn't convert immediately. Closes deals that were on the fence.

5

Dead Lead Revival

30/60/90 day re-engagement check-ins for leads that went cold. Situations change — AI is watching for when they do.

30 days Check-in: "Wanted to see how things are going with [their situation]."
60 days Re-introduce a relevant result story or new offering angle.
90 days Final revival attempt with a new hook. Many deals close here.
6

Seasonal Campaigns

Timely re-engagement based on calendar events that trigger buying decisions — sent to your full cold lead database.

New Year motivation, spring home improvement season, back-to-school buying windows, and other seasonal triggers are used to re-engage dormant leads at the moment they're most likely to take action.

FAQ

Questions about
AI Follow-Up Automation.

Won't leads find 7–10 follow-ups annoying?
Not when they're spread correctly across 14+ days, use different channels, and are genuinely personalized. The key is that each follow-up provides value or a new angle — not the same "just checking in" message sent repeatedly. Our sequences are designed to feel like a thoughtful person who genuinely wants to help, not a bot spamming the same message. Response rates are consistently 3–4x higher than manual follow-up.
What if a lead replies and wants to opt out?
Any opt-out request is handled automatically and immediately — the lead is removed from all active sequences and flagged as do-not-contact in your CRM. We are fully TCPA compliant on all SMS messaging and comply with CAN-SPAM for email. Opt-out rates on properly personalized sequences are typically under 1%.
Can I import my existing dead lead list?
Yes — and we recommend it. Many clients see their biggest ROI in the first 30–60 days by running our Dead Lead Revival sequence on their existing CRM contacts. We'll help you segment the list, build the appropriate re-engagement sequence, and give you a projected revenue recovery estimate based on your lead volume and average deal size.
How does AI know when to stop following up vs. escalate to my team?
The AI monitors every response for buying signals — questions about pricing, availability, timelines, or any language that indicates readiness to buy. When detected, the sequence pauses and your team gets an immediate notification with the full conversation context. For negative responses or opt-outs, the sequence stops automatically.
What CRMs and calendar tools does this integrate with?
We integrate natively with GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, and Zoho. For other CRMs, we connect via Zapier or Make webhooks. Every send, open, click, and reply is logged in your CRM in real time with no manual input required from your team.
