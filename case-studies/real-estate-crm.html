Real Estate Agent Tripled Pipeline With AI CRM

Lisa Chen was losing leads to faster-responding agents. With instant AI follow-up and a 7-touch nurture sequence, she went from reactive to first — and her pipeline tripled in a single quarter.

Client: Chen Properties · Lisa C. · Residential Real Estate

The Problem

Speed Kills in Real Estate — She Was Losing the Race

⏰

Leads Going Cold in Hours

In real estate, the first agent to respond wins the client. Lisa's average response time was 3-4 hours, and she was losing deals to agents who called back within minutes.

📂

Manual CRM Updates

After every call, showing, and email, Lisa had to manually log notes and update her CRM. This administrative burden consumed 40+ hours per month she could have spent with clients.

📩

No Nurture Sequence

Leads who weren't ready to buy immediately were forgotten. There was no system to stay top-of-mind until they were ready — so competitors captured them months later.

What We Built

First to Respond. Last One Standing.

Step 01

Instant Lead Response AI

The moment a new lead came in from any source — Zillow, website, referral — an AI responded within minutes with a personalized intro, qualifying questions, and a showing scheduler link.

Step 02

CRM Automation

Every email, call log, and showing note was auto-captured and synced to the CRM. Deal stages updated automatically based on buyer actions, giving Lisa perfect visibility with zero admin work.

Step 03

7-Touch Nurture Sequence

Leads not ready to buy immediately entered a 90-day nurture sequence — market updates, neighborhood guides, and gentle check-ins that kept Lisa top-of-mind until the buyer was ready.

The Outcome

First to Respond. Triple the Pipeline.
