How It Works

Start free. Upgrade when ready.

Most founders follow this path. You don't have to, but here's what typically works.

1

Sign up free — use all 20 tools

Start with idea validation, business planning, GTM strategy, and customer acquisition tools. All 20 are free, no time limit, no credit card. Take as long as you need.

Upgrade to Launch when you're ready to automate

Once you have customers and need to systemize your follow-up and CRM, Launch ($49/month) activates your first automation. Most founders upgrade here within 2–3 weeks.
