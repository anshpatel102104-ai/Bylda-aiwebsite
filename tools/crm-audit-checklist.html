CRM Health Audit Checklist

20 specific checks across 4 categories to find every revenue leak in your current CRM setup. Check off what you have — your score reveals where the gaps are.

🎯
Lead Capture
0/5
Every inbound lead source automatically enters your CRM
Website forms, Facebook ads, Google ads, and calls all flow into one system without manual entry.
All leads are tagged with their source at the point of capture
You can see which channels drive the best leads — not just the most leads.
Missed calls trigger an automatic text-back within 60 seconds
No lead is lost because they called after hours or during a busy period.
Lead capture forms include qualification fields (budget, timeline, service type)
You know within the first interaction whether a lead is high or low priority.
Duplicate leads are automatically merged or flagged
The same person contacting you twice doesn't create two separate records and two follow-up threads.
📱
Follow-Up Process
0/5
Every new lead receives an automated response within 5 minutes
No lead sits uncontacted. An SMS or email fires instantly when a new record is created.
You have a documented follow-up sequence with specific timing for each touchpoint
Day 0, Day 1, Day 3, Day 7 — not "whenever someone remembers to follow up."
Follow-up sequences use both SMS and email (multi-channel)
Relying on one channel alone means you'll miss leads who prefer the other.
Dead leads (30+ days, no response) are in a revival sequence
Old leads are a goldmine. A structured win-back campaign re-engages 5-15% of them.
Appointment reminders fire automatically 24 hours and 1 hour before each booking
Automated reminders reduce no-shows by 60% on average.
🔄
Pipeline Management
0/5
Your pipeline has clearly defined stages that reflect your actual sales process
Stages like "New Lead → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal → Closed Won/Lost" — specific, not generic.
Moving a lead to a new stage triggers an automated next action
When a lead moves to "Qualified," it automatically sends a booking link or schedules a task.
You review your pipeline at least once per week as a team
Leads don't go dark in an unreviewed pipeline stage. Regular reviews surface stuck opportunities.
Closed-lost leads have a documented reason and are in a nurture sequence
"Lost" doesn't mean gone forever. Timing-based revivals re-engage 10-20% of lost deals within 6 months.
Your CRM shows a clear dollar value for pipeline at each stage
You can see your weighted pipeline value and forecast revenue for the next 30, 60, 90 days.
📊
Reporting & Optimization
0/5
You track lead-to-appointment conversion rate by source monthly
You know which channels produce leads that actually convert — not just which drive volume.
You measure average response time to new leads
Speed-to-lead is one of the strongest predictors of conversion. If you don't measure it, you can't improve it.
SMS/email open and reply rates are tracked per sequence
You know which touchpoints are performing and which are being ignored — and you optimize based on data.
You have a dashboard showing daily/weekly new leads, appointments booked, and revenue
You don't need to run a report to answer "how's the business doing this week?" — the answer is visible at a glance.
You A/B test follow-up messages at least quarterly
Your best-performing templates today were found by testing. Active optimization compounds over time.
