v2.2 May 2025 Minor Release

KPI Dashboard + 3 New AI Tools

This release adds the long-awaited KPI Dashboard to Launchpad, giving founders a real-time view of their most critical business metrics. We also shipped three new AI tools: Revenue Forecaster, Competitor Watcher, and Offer Optimizer.

  • New KPI Dashboard with 12 configurable metric cards and live refresh
  • New Revenue Forecaster AI tool — predict next 90 days of revenue
  • New Competitor Watcher — monitor competitor pricing and positioning
  • New Offer Optimizer — AI-powered pricing and positioning suggestions
  • Improved Dashboard loading speed reduced by 60% across all plans
  • Fixed CRM sync occasionally dropping custom field values on update
v2.1 April 2025 Minor Release

Voice AI Improvements + Mobile App Beta

Major upgrades to our Voice AI system with significantly reduced latency and more natural conversation flow. We also launched the Launchpad mobile app in beta for iOS and Android.

  • New Launchpad mobile app (iOS + Android) — beta access
  • Improved Voice AI response latency reduced from 2.1s to 0.8s average
  • Improved Voice AI now supports 12 languages including Spanish, French, German
  • Improved Missed call text-back now triggers within 8 seconds (was 30s)
  • New Conversation sentiment analysis in AI call summaries
  • Fixed Twilio webhook occasionally not firing for missed calls on some area codes
  • Fixed Calendar booking conflicts when using multiple time zone rules
v2.0 March 2025 Major Release

Launchpad Launch — 20 AI tools

The biggest release in Bylda history. We launched Launchpad — our complete AI toolkit for founders — with 20 AI-powered tools covering idea validation, customer research, pitch creation, and business planning. This is the release that changes everything.

  • New Launchpad platform — 20 AI tools in one unified dashboard
  • New Idea Validator — validate any business idea against market data
  • New Pitch Deck Builder — AI-generated investor presentations
  • New ICP Builder — define your ideal customer profile with AI
  • New Cold Outreach Generator — personalized outreach at scale
  • New Business Model Canvas — one-page business strategy generator
  • New Free tier — 6 tools available with no credit card
  • Improved Completely redesigned UI with Space Grotesk + Inter typography system
v1.8 February 2025 Minor Release

Missed Call Text-Back System

One of our most requested features: automated missed call text-back. When a lead calls and you don't answer, our system instantly sends a personalized SMS to re-engage them before they call a competitor.

  • New Missed call text-back — automated SMS within 30 seconds of missed call
  • New Customizable text-back templates per campaign
  • New Two-way SMS conversation via AI after initial text-back
  • Improved GoHighLevel integration now syncs contacts bidirectionally in real time
  • Fixed Rare issue where appointment reminders sent twice for some calendar providers
v1.5 January 2025 Minor Release

CRM Pipeline Automation

Full CRM pipeline automation is now live. Leads automatically move through stages based on behavior triggers — no manual updates required. Connect your existing CRM or use our built-in pipeline.

  • New CRM pipeline automation with 12 configurable behavior triggers
  • New Lead scoring algorithm — automatically prioritize your hottest leads
  • New Zapier and Make.com native integration
  • Improved Follow-up sequence editor with drag-and-drop reordering
  • Improved Reporting dashboard with 30, 60, 90-day cohort analysis
v1.0 November 2024 Initial Launch

Bylda — Initial Launch

We launched. Bylda went live with our core done-for-you AI automation service, helping our first cohort of founders automate their lead generation and follow-up sequences. The beginning of everything.

  • New Bylda OS core service — done-for-you AI automation setup
  • New AI-powered follow-up sequences for leads
  • New Appointment booking automation
  • New Lead nurture email sequences
  • New GoHighLevel integration as primary CRM platform
  • New Onboarding portal and strategy call process
