The Truth

You already paid for these leads.
You're just not working them.

The average business has hundreds — sometimes thousands — of leads in their CRM that went cold after 1-2 attempts. They paid to acquire those leads through ads, referrals, or SEO. They spent time talking to them. And then they gave up. Meanwhile, those leads are still out there, still have the same problem, and are probably still looking for a solution.

A dead lead reactivation campaign doesn't require ad spend. It doesn't require new traffic. It's the highest-ROI thing most businesses aren't doing.

The Three-Phase System

How we wake up
your cold CRM.

1
Days 1–14

Re-Engagement Spike

High-frequency, high-impact touchpoints designed to break through inertia. Pattern-interrupt subject lines. Direct value propositions. A compelling, time-sensitive reason to respond now. SMS and email coordinated for maximum attention. Most reactivations happen in this phase.

2
Days 15–45

Value Drop

For leads that didn't respond in Phase 1, we switch to a trust-building, objection-handling sequence. Case studies, testimonials, educational content, and FAQs that address every reason they might have hesitated. Gives before it asks. Converts leads who needed more information or confidence.

3
Days 46–90+

The Long Game

Monthly check-ins, seasonal messaging, and trigger-based reactivation for timing-dependent leads. Some people weren't ready 3 months ago — but they are now. The long game captures revenue that would otherwise be written off completely.
