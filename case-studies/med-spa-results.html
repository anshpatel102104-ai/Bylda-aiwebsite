Case Study 💆 Med Spa

Med Spa Recovered $47K in Dormant Revenue in 30 Days

NovaOps AI deployed a full reactivation stack for Rejuvenate Med Spa — turning 400+ cold leads into booked appointments and a flood of 5-star reviews, all on autopilot.

Client: Rejuvenate Med Spa  |  Owner: Sarah M.  |  Timeline: 30 days

A boutique med spa in the Atlanta metro area with a busy front desk team, a growing client list, and a serious problem: hundreds of past leads who had gone completely dark.

The Challenge

A Growing Client List — With No Follow-Up System

Rejuvenate Med Spa was generating plenty of new inquiries, but couldn't keep up with manual outreach. Revenue was leaking through the cracks every single day.

🧊
400+ Leads Gone Cold
Over 400 past inquiries and consultation requests sat unanswered in their CRM. Without a structured follow-up system, each one had simply been forgotten — representing thousands of dollars in unrealized revenue.
⏱️
Manual Follow-Up Was Impossible
The front desk team was already stretched managing daily walk-ins and phone calls. There was no capacity to manually re-engage hundreds of old leads on top of daily operations.
⭐
No Review Collection System
Happy clients left without being asked to leave a review. The spa had fewer than 20 Google reviews despite serving hundreds of clients — making them invisible to new local searchers.
The Solution

We Deployed a Full AI Revenue Recovery Stack

NovaOps built and launched a complete automation system in under 14 days — covering missed calls, cold lead reactivation, and review collection.

01
Missed Call Text Back — Instant Response
Every missed call triggered an instant AI text message within 60 seconds. The message introduced the spa by name, offered to help, and linked directly to an online booking page. No call was ever truly "missed" again.
02
AI Reactivation Sequences for Cold Leads
All 400+ dormant contacts were loaded into a personalized 5-step reactivation sequence over 21 days — mixing SMS, email, and AI-personalized offers. The sequence felt human, not automated, and drove a 17% re-engagement rate.
03
Post-Appointment Review Automation
24 hours after every completed appointment, clients received a warm, personalized follow-up text requesting a Google review. The sequence included a direct link, a thank-you message, and a gentle second reminder if no action was taken.
04
CRM Pipeline Integration
All lead activity, booking events, and review statuses were automatically logged to a clean CRM pipeline — giving the owner a live dashboard of revenue activity without touching any spreadsheets.
