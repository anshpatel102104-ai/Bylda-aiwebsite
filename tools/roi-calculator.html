How We Calculate Your ROI

We use industry benchmarks from thousands of service businesses to estimate your automation impact. The core assumptions are conservative and field-tested:

AI follow-up rate → 100% (up from your current rate)
Conversion rate improvement → 2–3× with immediate, consistent follow-up
No-show rate reduction → 60% fewer no-shows with automated reminders

Monthly recovery = (missed leads × AI conversion rate × job value) − current results
Annual ROI = (monthly recovery × 12) − annual NovaOps investment
