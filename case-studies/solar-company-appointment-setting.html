4.8× ROAS

California Solar Company 2× Their ROAS with AI Appointment Setting

A California residential solar installer was spending $8k/month on ads with a barely profitable return. Slow follow-up was costing them leads to faster-moving competitors. NovaOps AI fixed their appointment funnel — and their ROAS went from 2.1× to 4.8× in 30 days.

Residential Solar Installer California $8k/Month Ad Spend Show Rate: 42% → 78%
The Problem

$8k/Month in Ads, Half the Leads Wasted on Slow Follow-Up

Solar is an intensely competitive market in California. Homeowners fill out multiple forms and book consultations with multiple installers simultaneously. Speed-to-response determines who wins the deal. This company was losing that race consistently.

📉
42% consultation show rate. Of the prospects who booked a consultation, 58% didn't show up. This is common in solar — where there's no follow-up system, prospects forget, get cold feet, or simply accept a competing offer before the appointment. Empty consultation slots cost the closers time and the business money.
🐌
Slow lead follow-up losing leads to competitors. The sales team was manually calling leads from the previous day's form fills each morning. By the time they called, prospects had already spoken to 2-3 competing solar companies. In solar, the company that calls within 5 minutes wins the appointment 80% of the time.
💸
High cost per booked consultation from ad waste. At $8k/month and a 2.1× ROAS, the margins were razor-thin. The majority of the ad spend inefficiency was downstream — weak follow-up and no-show rate were destroying the revenue generated by what was actually solid ad creative and targeting.
The Solution

Speed-to-Lead + Show Rate Optimization Stack

NovaOps AI built a four-system stack designed specifically for high-ticket, consultation-based solar sales — eliminating the lag between lead arrival and first contact, and maximizing the show rate of every booked appointment.

✓
60-Second Lead Response — Every ad lead triggers an immediate personalized SMS within 60 seconds, 24/7. The message references the specific ad offer, asks a qualifying question, and offers two available consultation times. Response rate: 68% within the first hour.
✓
AI Qualification Flow — Before booking a consultation, the AI qualifies: homeowner vs. renter, roof type, monthly electric bill, and credit range. Unqualified leads are filtered — qualified leads are fast-tracked to a booked slot. Closer time is reserved for real buyers.
✓
3-Touch Reminder Sequences — Every booked consultation gets three automated reminders: confirmation immediately after booking, a reminder 24 hours before with a video about what to expect, and a final day-of reminder with the closer's photo and name. Show rate jumped from 42% to 78%.
✓
CRM Pipeline Management — Full GoHighLevel pipeline setup: lead stages, automated task creation, dead lead reactivation, and closer performance tracking. The sales manager gained complete visibility into every stage of the funnel for the first time.
