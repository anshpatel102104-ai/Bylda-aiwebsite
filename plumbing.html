The Challenges

The problems holding your plumbing business back

🚨

Missed Emergency Calls

Emergency plumbing customers call one number, and if no one answers, they immediately dial the next plumber on Google. You lose the job before you even know it existed — often a $500–$2,000 job gone in 30 seconds.

📅

Manual Scheduling Headaches

Your office spends hours every day playing phone tag to book, confirm, and reschedule jobs. This back-and-forth wastes time that could be spent on more valuable work, and mistakes lead to double-bookings and missed appointments.

🌟

No Review Collection System

Plumbing customers only remember to leave a review when something goes wrong. Without a proactive review request system after every successful job, your Google rating stays flat while competitors with automated review systems pull ahead.

How NovaOps Helps

Three systems that run your pipeline automatically

📲

Missed Call Textback

Every missed call — day or night — triggers an instant text message that captures the lead and starts a conversation. Emergency callers get an immediate response and a booking link. Clients recover an average of 10+ jobs per month that would have gone elsewhere.

🤖

AI Booking Bot

Our conversational AI collects job details, checks your availability, and books appointments without any human involvement. Customers book at 11pm on a Tuesday just as easily as Monday morning. Job bookings double on average within 60 days.

⭐

Review Automation

Two hours after job completion, an automated message asks customers how everything went. Satisfied customers are guided to Google in two taps. Our clients average a 90% review request completion rate — building a dominant local reputation month after month.
