FAQ

Questions from
chiropractic offices.

How does AI handle appointment reminders for chiropractic? +

Bylda sends automated SMS reminders at 72 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours before each appointment. If a patient needs to reschedule, AI handles that conversation automatically and updates your calendar. Your front desk only steps in for complex situations — the system handles everything else.

Can AI reactivate lapsed patients without being spammy? +

Yes — the reactivation sequences are personalized, spaced appropriately (30/60/90 days), and written to feel like a genuine check-in, not a mass blast. Patients regularly respond positively and re-book. We stop messaging anyone who doesn't engage after the sequence, so you never irritate your patient base.

Does this work with ChiroFusion, Jane App, or Genesis? +

Bylda is designed to work alongside your existing practice management software. We integrate with ChiroFusion, Jane App, Genesis, and other major EHR systems via API or data sync. You don't need to replace anything — our system layers on top of what you already use.

Is this HIPAA-compliant? +

Yes. All messaging, data storage, and workflow infrastructure meets HIPAA requirements. We sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every chiropractic practice we work with. Patient data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is strictly controlled.

How quickly can we get set up? +

Most chiropractic practices are fully live within 14 days. The onboarding process includes an intake call, integration setup, message sequence review, and a test run before going live. Our team does the heavy lifting — your team's time investment is minimal. You'll be seeing results in week one.
