Architecture

Read first. Write back later.

Bylda connects over OAuth, scoped to what it needs, and reads your calls and CRM activity. Your CRM stays the system of record. Writing behavioral context back into it is on the roadmap, not in the first release.

  • OAuth connection, scoped to what Bylda needs
  • Calls from your CRM, dialer or call recorder, or by manual upload
  • No migration, no nightly batch, no CSV
  • Write-back to your CRM Roadmap
