The Challenges

The silent revenue leaks killing your business

📵

Missed Calls = Missed Jobs

You're on a job site, on the roof, or under a sink — and your phone rings. You can't answer. That customer calls the next company on Google and books with them instead, permanently.

📋

Slow Estimate Follow-Up

You send a quote and then wait. Days go by. When you finally follow up, the homeowner has already signed with someone else. Most jobs are lost not on price but on speed of response.

🔄

No Repeat Customer System

Past customers are your cheapest new jobs — but without a system to re-engage them, they search Google again next time and end up with a competitor. You've already earned their trust; you just need to stay top of mind.

How NovaOps Helps

Three automations that fill your schedule

⚡

Instant Missed Call Textback

Automated Estimate Reminders
