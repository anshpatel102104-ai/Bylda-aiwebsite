Yes — the Starter plan is permanently free and includes access to core validation tools including Idea Validator and Kill My Idea. No credit card required. You can upgrade to a paid plan anytime to unlock the full 10-tool suite.
How is Launchpad different from ChatGPT?+
ChatGPT is a general-purpose AI that requires you to craft your own prompts and interpret raw output. Launchpad is purpose-built for founders — each tool has a structured input form, a specialized prompt system, and a formatted output designed for real decisions. You don't need to know how to prompt AI to get expert-level results.
What's the difference between Launchpad and Nova OS?+
Launchpad is for the build phase — getting from idea to first customers. Nova OS is for the operate phase — done-for-you CRM, lead capture, automated follow-ups, client onboarding, and reporting for businesses that are already up and running. Many founders use Launchpad first, then graduate to Nova OS once they have paying customers.
Can I use multiple tools on the same idea?+
Absolutely — and that's exactly how Launchpad is designed to be used. Validate your idea first, then use Kill My Idea to find weaknesses, run a Pitch Generator to frame the narrative, and finish with the GTM Strategy Builder and First 10 Customers Finder to plan your launch. Each output informs the next.
