How revenue share works

Some NovaOps plans include a performance-based revenue share component in addition to (or instead of) a flat monthly retainer. Here's exactly how it's calculated:

Rev share is calculated as a % of closed revenue attributable to NovaOps-assisted leads

A lead is "attributed" to NovaOps if: (1) it entered your pipeline through a NovaOps-connected source, AND (2) the first meaningful contact was made by the AI (follow-up SMS, voice call, or automated booking). Attribution uses a last-touch model — NovaOps gets credit for the most recent qualifying interaction before close.

Example calculation

Month's closed revenue from AI-assisted deals$42,000
Rev share rate (10%)10%
Flat retainer (already paid)$497/mo
Rev share owed$4,200

Rev share is reported monthly. You'll receive an attribution report by the 5th of each month covering the prior month's closed deals. Payment is due within 15 days of receiving the report.

Important: Rev share only applies to deals that actually close. Leads that are booked but don't convert, or deals that are refunded, are not included in the calculation. We never charge rev share on revenue you didn't collect.

Billing questions

When is my monthly payment charged?

Your subscription renews on the same calendar date each month as your sign-up date. If you signed up on the 15th, you're billed on the 15th of each month. You'll receive an invoice by email 3 days before each charge.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover) via Stripe. ACH bank transfers are available for annual plan subscribers. Crypto payments are not currently supported. Your card details are encrypted and stored securely by Stripe — NovaOps never stores raw card numbers.

How do I update my payment method?

Log in to your NovaOps dashboard → Billing → Payment Methods → Add Card. Your new card will be used for the next billing cycle. If your current card has failed, updating your payment method will trigger an immediate retry of the failed charge.

Are there overage charges if I exceed my lead limit?

On Starter (500 leads/month) and Growth (2,000 leads/month), leads processed beyond your plan limit are billed at $0.10/lead. You'll receive an email notification at 80% and 100% of your limit. You can also set a hard cap so no overages are ever charged — leads above the cap will queue until the next billing cycle.

Can I pay annually and get a discount?

Yes. Annual plans are billed upfront at 2 months free (equivalent to ~17% off). Contact us at ansh@nova-ops.space to switch to annual billing. Annual plans are eligible for ACH bank transfer.

Upgrades, downgrades, and cancellations

How do I upgrade my plan?

Upgrades take effect immediately. In your dashboard → Billing → Change Plan → select your new plan → confirm. You'll be charged the prorated difference for the remaining days in your current billing cycle. Example: if you're 10 days into a 30-day cycle on Starter ($497) and upgrade to Growth ($997), you'll be charged (~$167) for the remaining 20 days' difference.

How do I downgrade my plan?

Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Your current plan and all features remain active through the end of the cycle. If you downgrade to a lower lead limit, leads processed above your new limit in the next cycle will either be capped or billed as overages based on your settings.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel at any time from your dashboard → Billing → Cancel Subscription, or by emailing ansh@nova-ops.space. Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period — you'll retain full access until then. There are no cancellation fees. Note: cancelling stops future charges but does not trigger a refund for the current period unless you qualify under the 30-day guarantee (see below).

Refund policy

We stand behind our work. Here's our refund policy in plain English:

30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

If you're genuinely unsatisfied with NovaOps within the first 30 days of your go-live date (not sign-up date), you're eligible for a full refund of your first month's payment. To claim: email ansh@nova-ops.space with subject "Refund Request" before Day 30. Refunds process within 5–7 business days.

What is not refundable?

Month 2+ payments are non-refundable. Setup fees (if applicable) are non-refundable after the system build is complete. Revenue share payments, once paid, are non-refundable. Third-party costs (SMS message fees, phone number fees) are not refundable as they're passed through at cost.

What if there's a billing error?

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, email ansh@nova-ops.space within 30 days of the charge with your invoice number and a description of the issue. Confirmed billing errors are credited to your account or refunded within 7 business days.
