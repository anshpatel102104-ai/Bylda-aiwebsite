The Challenges

Where solar companies lose deals they've already won

🕐

Long Sales Cycle Attrition

Solar deals take 30–90 days from first contact to installation. At every stage — site visit, proposal, financing approval — there's a window where leads go cold and competitors swoop in. Most companies lose 60%+ of leads not to objections but to silence.

📄

Proposal Ghosting

You build a detailed savings analysis, send it over, and then wait. The homeowner is interested — they're just busy, distracted, or comparison shopping. Without intelligent follow-up, most proposals expire without a decision.

🔍

Lead Qualification Delay

Your best closers waste hours qualifying leads that turn out to be renters, have bad credit, or have north-facing roofs. Every hour spent on an unqualified lead is an hour not spent closing a $25,000 deal.

How NovaOps Helps

AI that moves every lead from curious to closed

📬

Multi-Touch Nurture Sequence

Every new lead enters a 60-day multi-channel nurture sequence — educational content about solar savings, utility cost comparisons, financing options, and social proof. Leads stay engaged even when your sales team isn't actively touching them. Pipeline value grows by 2.5x.

🤖

Proposal Follow-Up AI

After every proposal is sent, an automated sequence checks in at days 2, 5, and 10 with value-reinforcing messages — comparison savings breakdowns, tax incentive reminders, and urgency drivers. Close cycles shorten by an average of 35%.

✅

Auto-Qualification

Our AI collects homeownership status, roof age, average utility bill, and credit comfort level before the first human call. Your sales reps only talk to pre-qualified prospects. Call-to-close rates improve dramatically because every conversation starts from a position of fit.
