How It Works

Listen. Understand.
Execute.

Four movements, running continuously in the background of your week. You never start them, and you never maintain them.

01 · Listen

Silent capture across calls, meetings, and email. No bot in the room. No behavior change.

02 · Understand

Meaning, not keywords. Signals, objections, committee, and risk — scored against the deal.

03 · Execute

CRM written. Tasks closed. Follow-ups drafted. Meetings proposed. Before you're off the call.

04 · Prepare

Tomorrow assembled overnight, ranked by impact, with the work already drafted.

ACT 01

It joins. Nobody notices.

Bylda connects to your calendar and conferencing tools once. From then on it is simply present — capturing audio, transcript, and context without a notetaker bot announcing itself or a rep remembering to switch anything on.

  • Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams
  • Email threads and calendar context, in the same timeline
  • Consent and recording rules enforced per region

Language becomes structure.

"Finance signs off, not me" is not a note. It is a decision maker, a new stakeholder, a changed close date, and a risk. Bylda converts sentences into the fields your pipeline actually runs on.

  • Objections classified and matched to proven responses
  • Decision makers detected the moment they're named
  • Risk raised from what was not said

ACT 03

The CRM writes itself.

Bylda holds write access to the systems you already run. It maps meaning to your fields — including your custom ones — and updates them within seconds of the call ending.

  • Standard and custom fields, mapped to your schema
  • Stage transitions with a written justification attached
  • Tasks closed automatically when the work is done
  • Every write reversible, attributed, and logged
