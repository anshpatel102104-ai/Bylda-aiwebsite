Common Questions

Is the audit really free?
Yes — 100% free with no strings attached. We do these audits because they create genuine value and help us understand your business. Whether or not you become a client, you'll leave with actionable insights.
How long does the audit take?
The call itself is 30 minutes. Before the call, we'll ask you to fill in a short intake form (5 minutes). After the call, you'll receive your AI Readiness Report via email within 24 hours.
Do I need to have any tech set up already?
Not at all. Whether you're running on spreadsheets and sticky notes or an enterprise CRM, the audit is valuable. We meet you exactly where you are and show you what's possible from there.
