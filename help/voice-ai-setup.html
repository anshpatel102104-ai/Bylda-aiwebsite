Voice AI setup guide

Build an AI voice agent that answers missed calls, qualifies leads, and books appointments — 24 hours a day, without your team lifting a finger.

Voice agentKnowledge baseCall routingEscalation rules

How NovaOps Voice AI works

When a call comes in to your business number and isn't answered within your configured ring time (default: 2 rings), NovaOps Voice AI picks up. The AI greets the caller, introduces itself, collects information, qualifies the lead, and either books an appointment directly or logs the call and sends your team an alert. The full call is transcribed and added to your CRM automatically.

Step 1: Build your AI voice agent

1

Choose your agent's name and voice

Give your AI agent a name (e.g., "Alex" or "Nova"). Select a voice from our library — options include natural-sounding US English voices in various tones (friendly, professional, warm). You can preview each voice before selecting.

2

Write the greeting script

The greeting is what your agent says when it picks up. Keep it under 15 seconds. Be upfront that the caller is speaking with an AI. Example:

"Hi, thanks for calling [Business Name]! This is Nova, an AI assistant. I'm here to help you get connected with the right person and answer any questions. Can I get your name first?"
3

Define qualification questions

Set the questions the AI asks to qualify the lead. These are asked conversationally — not like a form. Typical qualification questions:

# Real estate example 1. "Are you looking to buy, sell, or both?" 2. "What's your target timeline — are you looking in the next 3 months or longer term?" 3. "Have you been pre-approved for financing yet?" 4. "What area or neighborhoods are you focused on?" # Home services example 1. "What kind of work are you looking to get done?" 2. "What's the address for the job?" 3. "Is this urgent or are you planning ahead?"
4

Set call objective

Choose the primary goal of each call: Book an appointment (AI attempts to schedule before hanging up), Collect lead info only (captures details and ends call), or Warm transfer to a live agent (after qualifying, bridges to a human). You can set different objectives for business vs. after-hours calls.

Step 2: Build your knowledge base

The knowledge base is what your AI agent draws from when callers ask questions. A thorough knowledge base means fewer "I don't know" responses and more confident, accurate answers.

Services and pricing

List every service you offer with a brief description. Include general price ranges if you share them with callers (e.g., "Roof inspections start at $X" or "We offer free estimates"). If you don't quote prices on calls, instruct the AI to say "pricing varies and your specialist will discuss options with you."

Common objections and how to handle them

Provide scripted responses to the 5–10 most common objections: "I need to talk to my spouse," "I'm just getting quotes," "Your price is too high," "I'm not sure I'm ready." The AI will adapt these to conversational context.

Business information

Hours of operation, service areas, location/address, how long you've been in business, certifications or licenses, number of completed jobs. Callers frequently ask these as trust-building questions.

What happens next

Describe your process after a call: "After booking, you'll receive a confirmation text," "A specialist will call you within 2 hours," "Your first appointment is a free no-obligation consultation." Set expectations clearly so leads don't feel anxious after the call ends.

Escalation phrases

List phrases that should always trigger a human transfer — angry language, legal threats, emergency situations, explicit requests to speak with a person. The AI monitors for these in real time.

Step 3: Configure call routing

Call routing determines where calls go before and after the AI handles them.

Inbound routing

Define ring time before AI picks up. 0 rings = AI answers immediately. 2 rings = AI answers if you don't pick up within ~10 seconds. Recommended: 2–3 rings for the best of both worlds.

After-hours routing

Configure a separate after-hours script. After-hours AI can still qualify and book appointments — the system simply uses your calendar availability to block morning-only bookings.

Overflow routing

When your team is on other calls, AI picks up overflow. This ensures you never miss a lead during busy periods.

Warm transfer

For high-value calls, the AI can bridge to a live agent. The AI whispers a brief summary to the agent before the caller hears them.

Step 4: Set escalation rules

Escalation rules tell the AI when to hand off a call to a human. Getting this right is critical for both customer satisfaction and lead quality.

Angry caller If sentiment analysis detects frustration or aggressive language for 2+ exchanges, the AI apologizes and offers to transfer to a specialist immediately.
Transfer request Any variation of "let me speak to a human," "transfer me," "I want to talk to a real person" triggers an immediate warm transfer or a callback scheduling.
Emergency Keywords like "emergency," "urgent," "flooding," "fire," "immediately" bypass the normal flow and trigger your emergency escalation (e.g., on-call technician notification).
Unknown question If the AI encounters a question 3 times without a confident answer from the knowledge base, it acknowledges the limitation and offers a callback from a specialist.
High-value lead Configure lead score thresholds. Leads above a certain score (e.g., immediate timeline + high budget) can be automatically escalated for a live call while the lead is still on the phone.

Step 5: Testing your voice agent

Before going live, run through these 5 test scenarios by calling your configured number:

  1. Ideal lead: Act as a highly qualified prospect. Confirm the AI captures all info, attempts to book, and creates a CRM contact correctly.
  2. Objection handling: Push back with "I'm just getting quotes" or "I need to think about it." Confirm the AI handles this gracefully without giving up immediately.
  3. Transfer request: Say "I want to speak to a real person." Confirm escalation triggers correctly and you receive a notification.
  4. Unknown question: Ask something outside the knowledge base. Confirm the AI offers a callback rather than making something up.
  5. CRM sync: After the test call, verify the call transcript and lead data appeared in your CRM within 2 minutes of the call ending.

Call testing tip: Use a different phone than your business number to test. Your own number may be whitelisted and bypass certain routing rules. Use a friend's phone or a temporary number for the most accurate test.
