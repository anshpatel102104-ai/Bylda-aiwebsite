Our story

We believe founders deserve an unfair advantage.

Bylda exists to give ambitious founders the AI infrastructure that enterprise companies pay millions for — at startup prices.

Mission
Our mission is to eliminate the operational complexity that kills great ideas before they can become great businesses.

How it started.

Bylda was co-founded by Ansh & Dhruv after watching brilliant founders struggle — not with their ideas, but with the operational chaos of running a business. The tools were broken, the workflows were manual, and the systems were non-existent.

Every week, promising businesses were dying slow deaths. Not because of bad products, but because founders were drowning in follow-ups they forgot to send, leads that fell through the cracks, and admin work that consumed every waking hour.

The solution was obvious: AI automation. The infrastructure to run a business end-to-end, without headcount. The kind of system that enterprises spend millions building — made accessible to anyone with an idea and a laptop.

That's Launchpad. An AI business operating system that gives founders 20 free tools to launch their idea, and 6 done-for-you automation systems to run the business once they do. Built, configured, and live in 14 days — or it's on us.
