# Website Audit — usebylda.com

**Date:** 2026-07-25 · **Scope:** full static site (76 HTML pages), config, assets, and the Supabase backend the site writes to.

**Method:** the repo's own `maintenance/` toolkit (SEO audit + axe-core accessibility/overflow scan driven through headless Chromium), plus manual review of content, routing, CSS architecture, security headers, and database row-level security.

---

## Executive summary

The site is technically in good shape. SEO scores **92/100** with **zero critical issues**, row-level security is correctly configured on every database table, internal links all resolve, and the accessibility work from previous passes has largely held.

The problems that remain are mostly **not** technical. The single most serious issue is that a **pre-launch site** — every call-to-action on every page points to `/waitlist` — presents itself as an established business with **500 customer reviews, eight detailed client case studies with named cities and dollar figures, and press coverage from TechCrunch, Forbes, and Entrepreneur.** None of that can be true yet, and some of it carries real legal and search-penalty exposure.

Behind that sit three structural issues: the homepage redesign was never rolled out to the rest of the site (1 stylesheet vs 10), the site contradicts itself on core facts (pricing model, tool count, follower counts), and ~871 KB of dead assets plus several duplicate/orphan pages have accumulated.

## 1. Critical — fabricated social proof on a pre-launch site

Every CTA on the site points to `/waitlist` ("Join Waitlist", "Start Building"). The product has not launched. Against that, the site presents:

**1.1 `aggregateRating` structured data — search penalty risk**

`about.html:54` and `results.html:17` both embed:

```json
"aggregateRating": { "ratingValue": "4.9", "reviewCount": "500", "bestRating": "5" }
```

Google's structured-data policy requires ratings to come from genuine, verifiable reviews that are visible on the page. Fabricated or unverifiable ratings are grounds for a **manual action** that removes rich results site-wide. Separately, on `results.html` the rating is attached to a `WebPage` type, which is not a valid host for `aggregateRating` at all — so it is both non-compliant and invalid.

**Fix:** remove both `aggregateRating` blocks until there are real, displayed reviews.

**1.2 Fabricated press coverage — FTC and trademark exposure**

`press.html:178–193` presents three cards under the heading *"Stories and coverage from publications covering AI, entrepreneurship, and the future of business operations"*, attributed to **TechCrunch** (May 2025), **Entrepreneur** (April 2025), and **Forbes** (March 2025), each with an invented headline and excerpt.

This reads unambiguously as real coverage. Using major publications' names to imply endorsement that did not occur is deceptive advertising and trademark misuse. This was flagged in the previous audit (`POLISH-PROGRESS.md`) and never resolved.

**Fix:** delete the section, or replace it with a clearly-labelled "As we'd like to be covered" / media-kit framing that cannot be mistaken for real coverage.

**1.3 Eight fabricated case studies**

`results.html` is titled *"Client Results — Bylda | Real Founders, Real Revenue"* and contains eight case studies with specific, checkable-sounding detail:

- "Real Estate · Phoenix, AZ" — "no-show rate 40% → 18%"
- "$89k in pipeline recovered"
- "+$28k/month revenue", "renewal rate up 28%"
- Direct customer quotes: *"Bylda basically built us a sales machine. Our close rate doubled and our pipeline tripled."*

There is **no disclaimer** anywhere on the page (no "illustrative", "hypothetical", or "example" language).

These case studies are also **strategically stale** — they describe the previous local-services agency positioning (roofers, dentists, HVAC, missed-call text-back), not the current "AI growth platform for online founders" positioning.

**Fix:** rewrite as explicitly-labelled illustrative scenarios, or remove until real customers exist.

**1.4 Inconsistent social-proof numbers**

The site cannot agree on how many users it has: **"500+ founders"** (`results.html`, `blog/index.html`) vs **"3,000+ founders"** (`blog/index.html` newsletter box) vs **"500" reviews** in schema. Two contradictory numbers appear on the same page.

## 2. High — the site contradicts itself

**2.1 Two incompatible pricing models are both live and indexed**

`/pricing` presents a SaaS ladder: **Free Forever / Launch $49 / Operate $149 / Scale $299** per month.

Three other pages present an agency engagement model:

| Page | Price |
| --- | --- |
| `/pricing/done-for-you` | $4,997 |
| `/pricing/retainer` | $4,997 setup + $2,497/mo |
| `/pricing/rev-share` | $0 |

These three pages are **orphaned** (nothing on the site links to them) but they **are listed in `sitemap.xml`** — so they are submitted to Google, indexable, and reachable from search while presenting pricing that contradicts the main pricing page by two orders of magnitude.

**Fix:** delete the three legacy pricing pages and remove them from the sitemap, or reconcile them with the current model.

**2.2 The product's tool count changes page to page**

"**20** AI tools" (19 occurrences) vs "**14** AI tools" (9 occurrences) vs "**6** AI tools" (4 occurrences). The two Launchpad pages disagree in their own `<title>` tags:

- `launchpad.html` → "Launchpad — **20** Free AI Tools for Founders"
- `launchpad-bylda.html` → "Launchpad — **14** Free AI Tools for Ambitious Founders"

**2.3 The product has two names**

"**Bylda OS**" (69 occurrences) and "**Bylda OPS**" (25 occurrences) are used interchangeably for the same product. `press.html:193` also contains the find/replace artifact "**Bylda's Bylda OPS tier**" left over from the NovaOps → Bylda rebrand.

**2.4 Two different sets of social profiles**

- `twitter.com/byldaai` (×2) vs `twitter.com/usebylda` (×1)
- `linkedin.com/company/bylda-ai` (×2) vs `linkedin.com/company/bylda` (×1)
- `instagram.com/usebylda` (×1)

At least one set points at accounts that don't exist. The `Organization` schema also ships `"sameAs": []` — an empty array that should either list the real profiles or be dropped.

**2.5 Two different contact emails**

`ansh@usebylda.com` (137 occurrences) vs `hello@usebylda.com` (1). A personal address as the primary site-wide contact is also a scaling/credibility choice worth revisiting.

## 3. High — three newsletter forms silently discard signups

Three of the site's five forms do not send data anywhere.

**3.1 Homepage footer (`index.html:675`) — fakes success**

```html
<form onsubmit="event.preventDefault();this.querySelector('input').value='';
      this.querySelector('button').textContent='✓';">
```

The form clears the field and shows a **checkmark**. The visitor believes they subscribed. Nothing is transmitted or stored. This is the worst of the three — it actively misleads.

**3.2 Blog index (`blog/index.html:717`) — does nothing**

```html
<form class="newsletter-form" onsubmit="return false;" aria-label="Newsletter signup">
```

Clicking "Subscribe" produces no result and no feedback, under copy promising *"delivered every Tuesday. Join 3,000+ founders."*

**3.3 Blog sidebar category nav is entirely dead**

`blog/index.html:657–682` — all six "Browse by Category" links are `href="#"` with no JavaScript bound to `.sidebar-cat-item` (verified: zero matches across all JS). The post counts shown are hardcoded and fabricated: **12 + 9 + 14 + 7 + 11 + 15 = 68 posts**, against **25 actual blog posts**.

**The working forms exist** — `/free-audit` and `/newsletter` both POST correctly to `launchpad-api…workers.dev/api/form-submit`, and `/contact` POSTs to an n8n webhook. Ironically **`/newsletter`, the page with a working form, is orphaned** — nothing links to it, while the two fake forms sit on the two highest-traffic pages.

**Fix:** point the homepage and blog forms at the same `/api/form-submit` endpoint `/newsletter` already uses.

## 5. Medium — the redesign stopped at the homepage

The homepage was rebuilt on a single consolidated stylesheet. No other page was migrated.

| Page | Stylesheets | External CSS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `index.html` | **1** | **20 KB** |
| `about.html` | 10 | 197 KB |
| `pricing.html` | 9 | 181 KB |
| `services.html` | 9 | 181 KB |
| `results.html` | 8 | 174 KB |
| `faq.html` | 8 | 174 KB |
| `blog/index.html` | 8 | 170 KB |

Every non-homepage view makes **8–10 render-blocking CSS requests** for ~10× the payload.

**The layers are held together with `!important`:**

| Stylesheet | `!important` count |
| --- | --- |
| `bylda-polish.css` | 244 |
| `bylda-omni.css` | 187 |
| `bylda-redesign.css` | 95 |
| `bylda-light.css` | 83 |
| `mobile-optimize.css` | 46 |

That is **~655 `!important` declarations** across the override layers. Each redesign was stacked on top of the previous one rather than replacing it. This is why the homepage redesign could not be rolled out cheaply, and it makes every future style change progressively riskier.

**5.1 The homepage also has a different navigation from every other page**

`index.html` does not load `bylda-nav.js` (the shared mega-menu) — **70 of the other 75 pages do**. The homepage ships a bespoke nav whose links are same-page anchors (`#how`, `#loop`, `#everything`, `#types`), so a visitor moving between the homepage and any inner page sees the navigation change under them.

The other five pages without the shared nav are `/book-call`, `/sign-in`, `/thank-you`, `/waitlist` (all intentionally chrome-free conversion or redirect pages) and `careers/index.html` — the shadowed duplicate from §4.1, which is further evidence it is unmaintained.

(The homepage's own mobile burger menu is correctly wired at `index.html:703` — that part works.)

## 6. Medium — security headers are configured but not applied

`_headers` defines a solid policy — CSP, `Referrer-Policy`, `X-Frame-Options`, `X-Content-Type-Options`.

**That file is Netlify/Cloudflare Pages format. This site deploys to Vercel** (`vercel.json`, no `netlify.toml`), which ignores `_headers` entirely. The same applies to `_redirects` (97 lines of Netlify redirect syntax) — superseded by the 35 redirects in `vercel.json`.

So in production the site actually serves only what `vercel.json` declares:

- ✅ `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`
- ✅ `X-Frame-Options: DENY`
- ⚠️ `X-XSS-Protection: 1; mode=block` — deprecated; modern guidance is to send `0` or omit it, since the legacy auditor it enables can itself introduce vulnerabilities
- ❌ **No** `Content-Security-Policy`
- ❌ **No** `Strict-Transport-Security`
- ❌ **No** `Referrer-Policy`
- ❌ **No** `Permissions-Policy`

**Fix:** port the `_headers` policy into `vercel.json`, then delete `_headers` and `_redirects` so the repo stops implying protection it doesn't have.

One live discrepancy to resolve during that port: the CSP in `_headers` allows `connect-src` to `workers.dev` and `supabase.co` but **not** `n8n.usebylda.com`, which is where `contact.html:250` submits. Porting the policy as-written would break the contact form.

## 7. Backend security — clean, with two notes

Checked the live Supabase project (`BYLDA`, `ipidfqwlszuhjgjygbvx`) that `waitlist.html` writes to directly from the browser.

**The public `anon` key committed in `waitlist.html:558` is safe to be public** — that is what anon keys are for — and the table policies back it up correctly:

```
waitlist_public_insert   INSERT  {anon,authenticated}  WITH CHECK (status='waiting' AND user_id IS NULL)
waitlist_admin_select    SELECT  {authenticated}       USING is_admin()
waitlist_admin_update    UPDATE  {authenticated}       USING is_admin()
waitlist_admin_delete    DELETE  {authenticated}       USING is_admin()
```

Anonymous visitors can insert a constrained signup row and nothing else. **Signups cannot be read back by the public.** RLS is enabled on **all 140 tables**. There are **no ERROR-level security advisories.**

Two warnings worth acting on:

- **`anon_security_definer_function_executable`** — `public.unlock_next_course_module()` is executable by the `anon` role as `SECURITY DEFINER` via `/rest/v1/rpc/`. Anyone can call it unauthenticated with the function's elevated privileges. Revoke `EXECUTE` from `anon` or switch it to `SECURITY INVOKER` unless that is deliberate.
- **`rls_policy_always_true`** — `form_submissions_public_insert` allows unrestricted `INSERT`. Correct for a public form (reads are properly gated by `is_org_member`), but there is no rate limiting, so it is an open spam/abuse target.

Also: `auth_leaked_password_protection` is disabled (free HaveIBeenPwned check — worth enabling), and the `vector` extension is installed in the `public` schema.

## 8. SEO detail — 92/100

Zero critical issues; internal links all resolve; `sitemap.xml` covers 64 URLs; JSON-LD is valid on 73 of 76 pages.

**Note on the tool's 9 "high priority" findings — 5 are false positives.** The audit script crawls files without applying `vercel.json`. It flags `/book-call` and `/sign-in` for missing `<h1>`, thin content, and duplicate titles, but both are deliberate stubs carrying `noindex`, a correct `canonical`, a meta-refresh, **and** a 301 in `vercel.json`. They are handled correctly.

**The genuine high-priority items are the duplicate-metadata ones**, all covered in §2 and §4.2:

- `/bylda` + `/bylda-os` — duplicate title *and* description
- `/waitlist` — title shared with the two redirect stubs

**Genuine recommended fixes:**

- **19 titles exceed 65 characters** — worst offenders are `/operations/follow-up-booking` (91), `/operations/automation-workflows` (88), `/operations/reporting-dashboard` (82), and several blog posts at 76–78.
- **6 meta descriptions are too long** — the **homepage is 236 characters**, which will be truncated in results; `/blog/scaling-without-hiring` is 198.
- **6 indexable pages are missing from `sitemap.xml`** — `/blog/ai-for-auto-repair`, `/blog/ai-for-med-spas`, `/blog/ai-for-roofing-companies`, `/bylda-landing`, `/bylda-os`, `/lp/ai-automation-lp`, `/preview`.
- **`JobPosting` schema on `/careers`** (AI Engineer, Full-Stack Developer, Growth Marketer, AI Automation Specialist) omits `datePosted` and `validThrough`. `datePosted` is required by Google; without `validThrough` the postings never expire and go stale in Google Jobs.

## What is working well

Worth stating plainly, because a lot has been fixed already:

- **Zero critical SEO issues** and **zero broken internal links** across 76 pages.
- **RLS enabled on all 140 database tables**, with the public-facing waitlist policy correctly scoped to insert-only.
- **Metadata coverage is near-total** — `canonical` on **76/76** pages, `og:title` on 74/76, valid JSON-LD on 73/76 (the three gaps are the two redirect stubs and `/waitlist`).
- **The NovaOps → Bylda rebrand is essentially complete** — zero occurrences of `NovaOps`, `ByldaOps`, or the old domain remain (only the single `press.html` artifact).
- **The mega-menu navigation renders consistently** across 70 pages from one shared script.
- **The accessibility work from previous passes held** — landmarks, heading order, and form labels are in place.
- **The homepage redesign itself is genuinely good** — 1 stylesheet, 20 KB, working mobile nav. It is the right target for the rest of the site.
