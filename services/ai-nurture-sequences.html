Most businesses spend all their money acquiring new leads while completely ignoring the people who already raised their hand. The average business has $14k-$40k sitting in their CRM in the form of cold, unworked leads. These aren't dead contacts — they're people who had timing issues, budget constraints, or just fell through the cracks. A well-built nurture system brings them back to life, at scale, automatically.

Features

Multi-channel nurture.
12× the touchpoints, zero the work.

📱

Multi-Channel (SMS + Email)

Reach leads where they actually respond. SMS for urgency and high open rates, email for depth and nurture. Both channels run in coordinated sequences — not random blasts.

⚡

Behavioral Triggers

When a cold lead opens an email, clicks a link, or replies to a text — the AI detects the signal and escalates automatically. Hot leads get fast follow-up. Cold ones stay in the sequence.

🎯

Lead Scoring

Every interaction is scored. Leads are ranked by engagement and readiness. When a cold lead reaches threshold, it surfaces to your team as a hot opportunity — with full context.

🗂️

Segment Routing

Leads are automatically sorted by source, product interest, age of lead, and engagement history. Each gets a sequence designed for their specific situation — not generic messaging.

✨

Personalization at Scale

Every message uses the lead's name, prior interactions, and service interest. Messages feel human and specific — not automated. Response rates are 4× higher than generic sequences.

⏳

Long-Game Sequences (90+ Days)

Most competitors stop following up after day 14. We keep going for 90 days or more. The biggest wins often come from leads that needed time — not a new pitch.

The Playbook

Three-phase revival.
Designed to convert.

1

Phase 1: Re-Engagement Spike (Days 1–14)

High-frequency touchpoints to shake the lead out of inertia. Pattern-interrupt subject lines, a compelling reason to respond now, and a low-friction call to action. Best performers get immediate follow-up.

2

Phase 2: Value Drop (Days 15–45)

Educational content, social proof, case studies, and testimonials that build trust and handle objections. Every message gives before it asks. This phase converts the leads who needed more information.

3

Phase 3: The Long Game (Days 46–90+)

Monthly check-ins, seasonal offers, and trigger-based reactivation for leads that are genuinely timing-dependent. Some of the biggest deals come from leads that just needed the right moment.
