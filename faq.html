Everything, answered plainly.

Including the questions with awkward answers.

Bylda is a behavioral sales intelligence platform. It audits every sales conversation your team has and the CRM activity around it, identifies the seller and buyer behaviors affecting revenue, and tells managers and reps specifically what to change. It sits on top of the CRM, dialer, and calendar you already run rather than replacing them.

A CRM records activity and stores what someone typed after the fact. Bylda measures the conduct inside the conversations that produced those records — how much discovery happened, how objections were handled, where pricing landed relative to decision criteria, who actually attended. Your CRM stays the system of record; Bylda is the system of intelligence on top of it.

Conversation intelligence analyses one call at a time and hands a human per-call metrics to interpret. Bylda treats behavior repeating across many calls, reps and outcomes as the unit of analysis, returns one ranked directive with the evidence behind it, and then uses what happened next to judge whether that recommendation was right. Bylda is not a call library, so many teams run both.
