What is MEDDPICC call analysis?

MEDDPICC call analysis is the practice of checking recorded sales conversations for evidence of each qualification element — Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Paper process, Identified pain, Champion and Competition — instead of trusting CRM fields reps complete after the call.

What Bylda tracks

On every call, automatically.

Observable conduct only — each finding links to the moment in the recording, so anyone can check it.

  • Which elements were evidenced on the call, with the timestamp
  • Elements marked complete in the CRM but never discussed
  • Which reps skip which elements most often
  • Elements most often missing in deals that later stalled
  • Whether pricing was discussed before decision criteria

Where it breaks down

Three patterns worth catching early.

Economic buyer assumed, never met

The champion speaks for the signer. The signer never attends a call.

Paper process discovered at the end

Procurement, security and legal steps appear after the verbal yes.

Pain stated, never quantified

The buyer names a problem; the call never establishes what it costs.

FAQ

MEDDPICC call analysis, answered.

What does MEDDPICC stand for?

Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Paper process, Identified pain, Champion and Competition — a qualification framework for complex B2B sales.

How do you know if reps are really using MEDDPICC?

Check the calls, not the CRM. Fields are often completed from memory or optimism. Evidence in the conversation — the buyer describing their decision process, the signer attending — is what shows the element was actually covered.

Which MEDDPICC element is skipped most often?

It varies by team, which is the point of measuring it. Economic buyer access and paper process are frequently assumed rather than confirmed, and both tend to surface late in deals that slip.

Does Bylda replace MEDDPICC training?

No. It shows where the framework breaks down in real calls, so training and coaching can focus on the elements your team actually skips.
