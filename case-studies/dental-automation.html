Case Study

Dental Practice Cut No-Shows by 45% in 6 Weeks

Bright Smiles Dental eliminated their manual reminder process, reactivated 120 lapsed patients, and generated $32K in new bookings — without hiring a single staff member.

Client: Bright Smiles Dental · Dr. James K. · General Dentistry

The Problem

A Practice Held Back by Manual Processes

📉

30% No-Show Rate

Nearly one in three appointments went unfilled, costing thousands each week in lost chair time and wasted staff preparation hours.

📞

Manual Phone Reminders

Staff spent hours each day calling patients to confirm appointments — time that could have been spent on patient care and front-desk operations.

💤

Idle Lapsed Patient List

Hundreds of past patients hadn't been contacted in over a year. The data existed, but there was no system to turn it into booked appointments.

What We Built

Three Systems. Fully Automated.

Step 01

Automated Reminder Sequences

We deployed a 48-hour, 24-hour, and 2-hour reminder sequence via SMS and email. Each message included a one-tap confirm or reschedule link — no phone calls needed.

Step 02

Patient Reactivation Campaign

A targeted multi-touch campaign went out to 300+ lapsed patients. Personalized messages highlighted their last visit and offered an easy rebooking link.

Step 03

Review Automation Post-Visit

After each completed appointment, patients automatically received a review request. Five-star reviews were directed to Google, negative feedback was captured privately.
