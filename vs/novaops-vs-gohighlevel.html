Our Verdict

If you're a service business owner who wants more booked appointments, faster lead response, and better follow-up without learning a new platform — NovaOps AI wins decisively. You get expert-built automation running in under two weeks, and a team optimizing it for you every month.

Most service business owners — roofers, med spa owners, real estate agents, insurance brokers — are best served by the done-for-you model. Your time is worth more solving client problems than debugging automations.
