The visibility problem isn't a reporting problem

Every revenue leader has lived the same Friday: the forecast call says commit, the CRM says proposal sent, and three weeks later the deal quietly dies. Nothing in the pipeline warned you, because the pipeline only knows what reps entered — and reps enter conclusions, not behavior.

The information you actually needed existed the whole time. The economic buyer never joined a call. The champion's replies slowed from same-day to same-week. The implementation objection came up three times and was answered with a discount. It just never became data.

You don't have a visibility problem. You have a data source problem. The source has been the rep's memory.

Bylda changes the source. It observes the deals directly — calls, email, meetings, CRM activity — and gives you a read on the pipeline that doesn't depend on anyone's discipline or optimism.

Pipeline reviews stop being interrogations

When every deal carries its own history — what changed, which concerns are open, who's engaged and who isn't — the review starts at the decision instead of the reconstruction. Reps stop preparing slides about their own pipeline. Managers stop asking questions the system can answer. The hour goes to judgment, which is the only thing the room is actually for.

And because Bylda writes the CRM itself, the reporting your board sees is finally built on the same reality your reps are living. No more two versions of the quarter — see what that does for RevOps.
