What is demo call analysis?

Demo call analysis reviews product demonstration calls for how closely the demo followed the problems the buyer described in discovery, how much of the call was rep monologue, how the buyer engaged, and whether the call ended with a decision step.

What Bylda tracks

On every call, automatically.

Observable conduct only — each finding links to the moment in the recording, so anyone can check it.

  • Which features shown map to problems the buyer described
  • Longest uninterrupted rep monologue
  • When buyer questions started — and stopped
  • Who attended versus who needs to decide
  • How the call ended: scheduled decision step or vague follow-up

Where it breaks down

Three patterns worth catching early.

The feature tour

Everything the product does, instead of the three things this buyer needs.

The disappearing buyer

Questions stop halfway through; the rep keeps presenting.

No decision step

The call ends on interest, not on who decides what by when.

FAQ

Demo call analysis, answered.

Why do sales demos fail to convert?

Commonly because the demo covers features the buyer didn’t ask about, runs as a long monologue, happens before discovery was complete, or ends without a scheduled decision step.

How long should a sales demo be?

Long enough to show what solves the buyer’s stated problems — often shorter than reps think. Watch for when the buyer stops asking questions; that’s usually where attention ended.

What should happen at the end of a demo?

Agreement on what the buyer needs to decide, who else must be involved, and a scheduled next step with a date.

Can you analyze screen-share demos?

Bylda analyzes the conversation — who spoke, what was asked and how the call ended — from the recording your conferencing tool already captures.
