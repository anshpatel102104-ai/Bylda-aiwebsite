What is objection handling analysis?

Objection handling analysis tracks the objections buyers raise across a team’s sales calls — price, timing, authority, competitor, status quo — and measures how reps respond: speed, length, whether the concern was investigated, and what happened next in the deal.

What Bylda tracks

On every call, automatically.

Observable conduct only — each finding links to the moment in the recording, so anyone can check it.

  • Objections by type, trend and rep
  • Response behavior: immediate answer, acknowledgement, diagnostic question
  • Reply length and pace after each objection
  • Objections left unresolved at the end of the call
  • Which responses appear before deals that progress

Where it breaks down

Three patterns worth catching early.

Answering the wrong objection

The rep responds to the stated objection instead of the concern underneath it.

Winning the argument, losing the deal

Long, well-reasoned rebuttals that end the buyer’s engagement.

Unresolved and forgotten

The objection is raised, parked and never returned to.

FAQ

Objection handling analysis, answered.

What are the most common sales objections?

Price, timing (“not right now”), authority (“I need to check with…”), competitor and status quo (“we’re fine with what we have”). Which dominates varies by market and changes over time.

What’s the best way to handle objections?

Acknowledge, investigate, then respond. Most objections hide a more specific concern, and a single diagnostic question usually does more than a scripted rebuttal.

How do you track objections across a sales team?

Tag every objection by type across all recorded calls and record what each rep did next. Manual tracking samples too few calls to see trends.

Which objection responses work best?

The honest answer is: the ones associated with better outcomes in your own deals. That’s measurable once every objection and response is tracked.
