Ohio HVAC Company Adds $89k Annual Revenue by Capturing Missed Calls

A residential and light commercial HVAC company in suburban Ohio was missing 35% of all inbound calls — most of them during after-hours emergencies and peak season surges. NovaOps AI deployed a 24/7 AI voice receptionist that eliminated every missed call and added 28 booked jobs per month.

Residential + Light Commercial HVAC Suburban Ohio 28 Additional Booked Jobs/Month 0 Missed Calls
The Problem

35% of Revenue Was Walking Out the Door Unanswered

HVAC is an emergency-driven business. When a furnace fails at 10pm or an AC unit dies on a 95-degree day, homeowners call the first number that answers. This company was letting 35% of those calls go to voicemail — and voicemails in HVAC almost never convert.

📵
Missing 35% of inbound calls during after-hours and peak season. With a small office staff working 8–5, every after-hours call went to voicemail. During summer and winter peaks when call volume spiked, daytime calls were also missed when all lines were busy. Emergency calls that went to voicemail were gone — homeowners would simply call the next number on Google.
🔄
No system for calling back missed calls systematically. Voicemail messages were handled reactively — someone would listen in the morning and try to call back. By then, many homeowners had already booked a competitor. There was no tracking of missed calls, no callback SLA, and no way to measure what was being lost.
The Solution

AI Receptionist + Instant Text-Back = Zero Missed Opportunities

NovaOps AI deployed a two-layer capture system: an AI voice receptionist that answers every call 24/7, and a missed call text-back as a failsafe. Together, they guaranteed that no lead ever went cold again.

✓
AI Voice Receptionist (24/7) — A conversational AI answers every call, gathers the customer's name, address, system type, and issue description, and books a service appointment directly on the technician's calendar. After-hours emergency calls are flagged for same-day dispatch. The AI sounds natural and professional — customers regularly don't realize they're talking to AI.
✓
Missed Call Text-Back — If any call slips through (during brief system transitions or high-volume bursts), the system automatically fires an SMS within 30 seconds: "Sorry we just missed your call — we have technicians in your area today. Reply to book or call us back." Response rate: 41%.
✓
24/7 Appointment Booking — The AI has full access to the scheduling system and can book, confirm, or reschedule appointments at any hour. This is especially powerful for commercial clients who often call off-hours and previously received no response until the next business day.
✓
CRM Logging & Dispatch Integration — Every call, text, and booking is automatically logged to the CRM with full context. Dispatchers arrive in the morning with a complete picture of overnight bookings, pending callbacks, and priority emergency jobs.
