Who It's For

We don't have a logo wall yet.
Here's the honest version.

Bylda is in early access. Rather than invent case studies, this page tells you exactly who the product fits, who it doesn't, and what an evaluation actually looks like.

Why this page isn't testimonials

Most early-stage sales tools put three case studies and eight logos on a page like this. You have read those pages. You know roughly how much of it to believe, and you have probably learned to skim past them to the pricing.

We would rather spend the page on something you can actually use: whether this is going to work for your team. If it isn't, finding that out here saves us both a call.

When we have customers willing to be named, they will be named, with numbers they agreed to. Until then, this page stays honest.

Fit

Where Bylda works,
and where it doesn't.

A good fit

  • ✓B2B sales teams of roughly 5–150 reps
  • ✓Calls are already recorded — Zoom, Meet, Teams, or a dialer
  • ✓A CRM is in use, even if the data in it is poor
  • ✓Managers have more reps than listening hours
  • ✓Deals involve discovery, objections, and more than one stakeholder
  • ✓Someone can name a performance gap they cannot currently explain

Not a fit yet

  • ✕Sales happens mostly over email or chat, not conversation
  • ✕Calls are not recorded and cannot be, for policy or legal reasons
  • ✕Transactional selling with little discovery — one call, one close
  • ✕Fewer than about five reps, where a manager genuinely hears everything
  • ✕You want a call recorder or a CRM — Bylda is neither
  • ✕You want guaranteed win-rate lift in writing. Nobody can honestly offer that

If you are in the right column, we will tell you on the first call rather than sell you a pilot that disappoints.

The evaluation

One week of real calls. That's the whole test.

No six-week pilot, no success criteria workshop. Either the findings are ones you would have reached yourself with unlimited listening time, or they aren't.

DAY 1 Connect OAuth to your conferencing, calendar, and CRM. Scoped, reviewed, reversible.
DAY 2 First audits Bylda reads the conversations that already happened. Nothing to schedule.
WEEK 1 Echoes appear Behaviors repeating across reps start separating from one-offs.
THE TEST Verify one Pick a finding you doubt. Listen to the timestamp. Decide if it is right.
THEN Your call If the findings are not useful, say so and we stop. No pilot to unwind.

The verification step is the one that matters. Every claim Bylda makes points at a moment you can check, which means you are never asked to trust the software — only to audit it once.

What we will and won't claim

Since this page exists in place of results, it should be clear about the difference between the two.

  • We will say what Bylda observed in your conversations, with the timestamp behind it, and whether a pattern is strong enough to act on or still too thin.
  • We will say which behaviors are associated with better outcomes in your own data, once enough outcomes have resolved to tell signal from noise.
  • We will not say that a behavior caused an outcome. Observed association is not proof of cause, and anyone selling you certainty on that is selling something else.
  • We will not say a number like "31% more meetings" unless it came from your data and we can show the working.
  • We will not invent a customer, a quote, or a logo. Which is why this page reads the way it does.

The same principle runs through the product: see what ships today versus what doesn't, and how the Behavior Graph accrues rather than arriving pre-trained.

Who you'll be talking to

Early access is small on purpose. You will work directly with the people building the product, the feedback loop is days rather than quarters, and the behavioral patterns we find in your market shape what gets built next. That is the actual trade in an early-access program, and it is worth more than a discount.

Bring a week of real calls.

Either the findings are useful or they are not. You will know inside a week.
