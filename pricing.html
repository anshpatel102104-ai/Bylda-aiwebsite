FAQ

Common Questions

What's the difference between Launchpad and Nova?
Launchpad is for the build phase — 17 AI tools to validate ideas, create pitches, map go-to-market, find customers, and generate content. Nova is for the operate phase — done-for-you CRM, lead capture, automations, follow-up, onboarding, and reporting systems. Launch plan unlocks 8 Launchpad tools. Operate plan adds 5 more tools plus Nova OS. Scale unlocks all 17 tools.
Can I start on Free and upgrade later?
Yes. The Starter plan is permanently free with access to 2 core validation tools. You can upgrade to Launch, Operate, or Scale at any time — your data and history carry over automatically.
How long does Nova OS setup take?
Typically 14 days from your onboarding call. We handle everything — you complete a 60-minute kickoff session, then we build and configure all six Nova systems for your specific business.
Do I need both Launchpad and Nova?
Not necessarily. Pre-launch founders often start with Launchpad only (Launch plan). Established businesses that already have a product skip straight to Nova (Operate plan). The Scale plan is for businesses ready to optimize and expand aggressively.
Is there a contract or commitment?
No long-term contracts. All plans are month-to-month and can be cancelled any time. Scale plan includes a 30-day minimum due to the custom build work involved.
How do I know this is worth $149/month?
One recovered appointment from a lead you would have lost pays for the Operate plan many times over. HVAC clients recover $3,000–$8,000 jobs that were going to voicemail. Real estate clients close deals worth $10,000–$20,000 in commission from leads their team never followed up on. The math is simple: if the system books even one qualified appointment per month that you would have otherwise missed, it has already paid for itself. Most clients see results in the first two weeks.
What if I don't see results in the first 30 days?
Nova OS plans include a written guarantee: if your system doesn't book at least 10 qualified appointments within 30 days of going live, we continue working at no charge until it does. This applies to the Operate and Scale plans. We put this in the service agreement — not just in the marketing copy — because we're confident in what we've built. If we can't deliver, you don't pay.
