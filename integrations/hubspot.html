What Bylda does with HubSpot

Bylda connects to HubSpot over OAuth, reads your pipelines, deal stages, and properties — custom properties included — and proposes a mapping you approve before anything is written. Then the maintenance disappears:

  • Deals stay current. Stage moves with the justification, close dates from what was actually said, amounts and next steps that match the conversation.
  • Contacts and associations build themselves. New stakeholders are created and associated the moment they're mentioned, with their role in the deal.
  • Activity logs itself. Calls, emails, and meetings land on the timeline as structured engagements with summaries — not pasted transcripts.
  • Everything is reversible. Each write is logged, attributed to its source moment, and undoable from the audit trail.
HubSpot's own automation moves records when rules fire. Bylda decides what the record should say in the first place.

Intelligence on top of clean data

Workflows and reports get better the moment the data underneath them is real. But Bylda goes further than clean properties: it reads the behavior across your deals — the objection raised twice, the champion gone quiet, the decision maker who never joined — and turns it into flags and next moves your team can act on. That's deal intelligence, running on the HubSpot you already have.
