Independent Realtor Recovers $180k Pipeline from Dead Leads

An independent realtor had over 200 leads sitting in a spreadsheet with no follow-up. NovaOps AI built a complete CRM and nurture system that cut response time from 47 hours to 45 seconds — and recovered $180k in pipeline from leads they'd written off.

Independent Real Estate Agent Suburban Market Buyer + Seller Leads 4× Appointments Booked
The Problem

Losing Listings to Faster-Responding Agents

Real estate is a speed-to-lead business. The first agent to call back almost always gets the appointment. This realtor was averaging 47 hours — and watching listings go to competitors who called first.

⏰
Response time averaging 47 hours. Between showings, closings, and client meetings, returning new leads promptly was nearly impossible. By the time a callback happened, the prospect had already toured properties with another agent — or listed with someone who answered faster.
📊
200+ leads in a spreadsheet with zero follow-up. Years of buyer and seller leads had accumulated in a Google Sheet. No CRM, no tags, no pipeline stages, no follow-up sequences. The majority of these leads had received one initial call — and then nothing. They represented hundreds of thousands in potential commission.
🏆
Losing listings to faster-responding competitors. In the suburban market, competing agents had VA-supported follow-up or team structures. As a solo agent, competing on speed was impossible without a system. Multiple sellers mentioned they went with another agent "because they called back first."
The Solution

CRM + AI Follow-Up That Works Like a Full Sales Team

NovaOps AI deployed a complete Go High Level CRM setup with buyer and seller-specific nurture sequences, dead lead revival campaigns, and instant AI-powered response that made this solo agent compete like a top-producing team.

✓
Instant Lead Response — Every new lead from any source (Zillow, Realtor.com, website, referral) triggers a personalized AI SMS and email within 45 seconds. The message references the specific property or search they inquired about and offers three available showing times.
✓
GHL CRM Setup & Pipeline Management — Full CRM buildout with buyer and seller pipelines, lead source tracking, deal stage automation, and task creation for human touchpoints. The spreadsheet chaos was replaced with a clean, automated system.
✓
Buyer/Seller-Specific 90-Day Nurture Sequences — Separate drip campaigns for buyers and sellers: market updates, buyer guides, home valuation content, and local neighborhood reports — all personalized and automated based on lead behavior.
✓
Dead Lead Revival Campaign — All 200+ dormant leads in the spreadsheet were imported and tagged. A structured reengagement campaign was launched: 6 touches over 45 days, including a personalized market update and a "your home may now be worth more" hook. 23 leads re-engaged and booked consultations.
